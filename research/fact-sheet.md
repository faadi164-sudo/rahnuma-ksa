# Fact sheet: sourced data behind the guides

Checked 10 November 2026. Every guide is `draft: true` until you verify its facts against the official portal and set `draft: false`.

**Confidence**
- **A** = official source (ministry website or portal) or a Saudi Gazette report quoting the ministry/Jawazat.
- **B** = reputable press or law firm reporting the official rule.
- **C** = commonly listed by expat sites; confirm on the portal before publishing.

## Exit re-entry and final exit

| Fact | Value | Source | Conf. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single exit re-entry | SAR 200 for ≤2 months + SAR 100 per extra month (within iqama validity) | [Gulf News 2025](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-no-refunds-for-canceled-exit-and-re-entry-visas-1.500163506), SG 628476 | A/B |
| Multiple exit re-entry | SAR 500 for ≤3 months + SAR 200 per extra month | same | A/B |
| Extension from abroad | Doubled: SAR 200/month single, SAR 400/month multiple | same | A/B |
| Fee refund on cancellation | None | Gulf News 2025 (Jawazat statement) | B |
| Conditions | Traffic fines paid; no valid unused visa; inside KSA; passport ≥ 90 days; fingerprints | [SG 639701](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/639701) | A |
| 3-year ban for not returning | Lifted from 16 Jan 2024 | SG 639701; Deloitte | A |
| Worker can request own exit re-entry on Absher | Yes (Labor Reform Initiative, Mar 2021); employer objection window | Global Compliance News; MHRSD LRI guidebook | B |
| Newborn without iqama | Cannot get exit re-entry | Middle East Briefing (Jawazat conditions) | B |
| Exit re-entry → final exit while abroad | Not allowed | KSA Expats citing Jawazat | C |
| Final exit conditions | Dues cleared (incl. phone bills); no vehicle; no unused visa; passport ≥ 60 days | [SG 607321](https://www.saudigazette.com.sa/article/607321) | A |
| Final exit validity | 60 days, not extendable; no visa fee | SG 607321 | A |
| Final exit cancellation fine | SAR 1,000 if worker doesn't leave | SG 607321 | A |
| Worker-requested final exit | If employer doesn't respond in 10 days → final exit valid 15 days | SG 607321 | A |
