Checked 10 November 2026. Every guide is `draft: true` until you verify its facts against the official portal and set `draft: false`.

**Confidence**
- **A** = official source (ministry website or portal) or a Saudi Gazette report quoting the ministry/Jawazat.
- **B** = reputable press or law firm reporting the official rule.
- **C** = commonly listed by expat sites; confirm on the portal before publishing.
