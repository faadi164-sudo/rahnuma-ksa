## Step by step

1. **Fill the form.** Register on the Global Passport Seva portal, choose **re-issue**, complete the form and print it.
2. **Book VFS.** Book an appointment at your nearest VFS Global centre on its India–Saudi Arabia page.
3. **Gather documents:**
   - [ ] Printed application form.
   - [ ] Three 2 × 2 inch photos with a white background.
   - [ ] Original passport and copies of its key pages.
   - [ ] Copy of your valid iqama.
4. **Attend the appointment.** Submit documents, give biometrics and pay.
5. **Collect** the new passport from the centre, or pay for courier delivery.
6. **Update Absher.** Your employer updates your passport for your own iqama; you update your sponsored family members'.

## Fees (from 1 July 2026)

| Service | Normal (SAR) | Tatkal (SAR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 36 pages, adults and ages 15–18 (re-issue) | 460 | 919 |
| 60 pages, adults and ages 15–18 (re-issue) | 644 | 1,103 |
| Minor below 18 | 331 | 791 |
| 36 pages in place of lost or damaged | 919 | 1,379 |

Add the Indian Community Welfare Fund charge (SAR 8) and the VFS service fee per application.

> Check the Embassy of India, Riyadh or Consulate General in Jeddah fee page before you pay. Fees were revised in July 2026.

## Common mistakes

- Photos that don't meet the specification.
- Forgetting the iqama copy.
- Booking travel before the new passport is updated in Absher.
