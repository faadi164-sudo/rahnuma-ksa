## Riyadh or Jeddah: which mission is yours

| Mission | Covers | VFS centres |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Embassy of India, Riyadh | Central, Eastern and Northern regions | Riyadh (Umm Al Hamam), Al Khobar, Buraidah, Hail, Jubail and Sakaka; passport services only at Arar, Hafr Al Batin, Hofuf, Al Khafji, Wadi Al Dawasir and Al Qurayyat |
| Consulate General of India, Jeddah | Western and Southern regions | Jeddah (Hail Street), Makkah, Madinah, Abha/Khamis Mushait, Jazan and Najran |

Main centres take applications 08:30–15:00, Sunday to Thursday (09:00–15:00 at some smaller centres). Check the address on the Embassy or Consulate website before you go.

## Step by step

1. **Fill the form.** Register on the Global Passport Seva portal, choose **re-issue**, complete the form and print it.
2. **Book VFS.** Book an appointment at your nearest VFS Global centre on its India–Saudi Arabia page.
3. **Gather documents:**
   - [ ] Printed application form.
   - [ ] Three 2 × 2 inch photos with a white background.
   - [ ] Original passport and copies of its key pages.
   - [ ] Copy of your valid iqama.
4. **Attend the appointment.** Submit documents, give biometrics and pay.
5. **Collect** the new passport from the centre, or pay for courier delivery.
6. **Update Absher.** Your employer updates your passport for your own iqama; you update your sponsored family members'.

## Fees (from 1 July 2026)

| Service | Normal (SAR) | Tatkal (SAR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 36 pages, adults and ages 15–18 (re-issue) | 460 | 919 |
| 60 pages, adults and ages 15–18 (re-issue) | 644 | 1,103 |
| Minor below 18 | 331 | 791 |
| 36 pages in place of lost or damaged | 919 | 1,379 |

Add the Indian Community Welfare Fund charge (SAR 8) and the VFS service fee (SAR 13). A normal 36-page renewal therefore costs SAR 481 in total, or SAR 940 with Tatkal.

Optional VFS extras include form filling (SAR 30), photos (SAR 15), courier delivery (SAR 45) and SMS updates (SAR 1).

> Check the Embassy of India, Riyadh or Consulate General in Jeddah fee page before you pay. Fees were revised in July 2026.

## Tatkal (urgent) renewal

- **Riyadh jurisdiction:** walk in at the Riyadh or Dammam VFS centre, Sunday to Thursday, 09:00–12:30. No appointment is needed.
- **Jeddah jurisdiction:** only at the Jeddah centre on Hail Street.
- The passport is normally issued within three working days, not counting the day you apply.
- Tatkal isn't available for lost passports.

## Lost, stolen or damaged passport

For a lost or stolen passport, bring:

- [ ] The online application form.
- [ ] A sworn affidavit (Annexure F).
- [ ] A Jawazat or police report of the loss, with a certified English translation.
- [ ] A Jawazat printout of your iqama details, also translated.
- [ ] Two photos, a copy of your iqama, and copies of the old passport if you have them.

A damaged passport needs the passport itself and the affidavit instead of the police report. Replacement costs SAR 919 for 36 pages or SAR 1,103 for 60 pages (minors SAR 791).

## After you get the new passport

- **Update Absher before you travel.** Your iqama and visas are linked to your passport number. Use **Update Resident's Passport Information**, or ask your employer to update it.
- **Check your exit re-entry visa** is on the new passport if you are going and coming back.
- **Mind the validity rules.** Jawazat issues an exit re-entry visa only if your passport has at least 90 days left, and a final exit needs at least 60 days. Renew before you fall below these.

## Common mistakes

- Photos that don't meet the specification.
- Forgetting the iqama copy.
- Booking travel before the new passport is updated in Absher.
