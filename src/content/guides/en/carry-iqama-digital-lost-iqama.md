## Do you have to carry it?

Yes, you must be able to prove your identity and residency. Since January 2023, Jawazat has said expats and their dependents can use the **digital iqama** on their phone instead of the card or a photocopy. You'll find it in the Absher Individuals app; Tawakkalna also shows your ID.

| What you carry | OK? |
| --- | --- |
| Physical iqama card | Yes |
| Digital iqama in Absher (or Tawakkalna) | Yes, inside Saudi Arabia |
| Photocopy or a photo in your gallery | Not a substitute |
| Nothing | Residency violation, fine |

The Saudi Gazette has reported the immediate fine for not carrying an iqama as SAR 1,000, and said people with only minor violations like this are not to be detained. Some guides cite higher penalties, so don't risk it.

## If you lose the card

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Fine for a lost iqama | SAR 1,000 |
| Replacement card when one year or less is left | SAR 500 |

These amounts come from Jawazat's answers as reported in November 2026. If the card was stolen, report the theft to the police and keep the report for your employer.

## Step by step: lost iqama

1. **Check your phone first.** Your digital iqama still works, so you can keep using it while the card is replaced.
2. **If it was stolen,** report it to the police and keep the report.
3. **Tell your employer (HR).** The employer reports the loss and requests a replacement through Muqeem. Family members' sponsors use Absher.
4. **Pay the fine and replacement fee** through government payments (SADAD) when the system issues them.
5. **Update your National Address** so the replacement card reaches you by post.

## Common mistakes

- Carrying a photocopy and thinking it counts.
- Letting your phone battery die when you've left the card at home.
- Paying an agent on social media who promises a new iqama "without the fine".
