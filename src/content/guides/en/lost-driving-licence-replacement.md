## Before you start

- [ ] **Valid iqama.**
- [ ] **No unpaid traffic fines** (check on Absher or Efaa).
- [ ] **Police report** if the licence was stolen.
- [ ] **The damaged card**, if you are replacing a damaged licence and visiting Muroor.
- [ ] Photos (4x6 cm) if you go to a Muroor office; they may ask for them.

## Step by step

1. **Pay the fee.** In your bank app, go to government payments (SADAD) → traffic → driving licence, choose the replacement for a lost or damaged licence, and pay **SAR 100** against your iqama number.
2. **Log in to Absher** and go to **My Services → Traffic services**.
3. **Choose the licence replacement service** for a lost or damaged licence, and follow the steps.
4. **If Absher doesn't offer it for your case,** book a Muroor appointment for the purpose "issue a replacement for a damaged or lost licence" and take your documents.
5. **Collect or receive the new card.** Track it in Absher. It may be handed over at Muroor or delivered by Saudi Post to your National Address.

## Need proof of your licence now?

Issue a **driving licence data report** in Absher: My Services → General Services → Absher Reports → driving licence data report. It can be printed and verified with a QR code. See our guide to Absher Reports.

## Common mistakes

- Paying the renewal fee instead of the replacement fee.
- Trying to apply with unpaid traffic fines.
- Not updating your National Address, so the new card goes to an old address.
