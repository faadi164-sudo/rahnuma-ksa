## The three fees at a glance

| Fee | Amount | Usually paid by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iqama (residence permit) fee for private-sector workers | SAR 650 a year, or roughly SAR 163 / 325 / 488 for 3 / 6 / 9 months | Employer |
| Work permit levy (expat levy, "Maktab Amal" fee) | SAR 800 a month (SAR 9,600 a year), paid for at least three months at a time | Employer |
| Dependent and companion fee | SAR 400 a month per person (SAR 4,800 a year) | The worker who sponsors them |

Firms whose expatriate staff do not outnumber their Saudi staff were set a lower levy rate when the levy was introduced. Check the amount shown on Qiwa or Muqeem for your employer.

Domestic workers (house drivers, maids) follow different rules: there is no work permit levy, and their iqama is normally renewed yearly.

## Who counts as a dependent or a companion

The SAR 400 monthly fee applies to both groups:

- **Dependents:** your wife, sons under 18 and daughters.
- **Companions:** sons aged 18 and over, additional wives, parents, parents-in-law, and domestic workers or other expatriates registered under you.

## Example: a worker with a wife and two children, for 12 months

| Item | Calculation | Total (SAR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worker's iqama fee | 650 | 650 (employer) |
| Work permit levy | 800 × 12 | 9,600 (employer) |
| Dependent fees | 400 × 12 × 3 people | 14,400 (worker) |

## How the fees are paid

1. **Your own iqama:** your employer pays the iqama fee and levy and renews your iqama on Absher Business, Muqeem or Qiwa. You do not pay these yourself.
2. **Your family's iqamas:** pay the dependent fee and iqama fee through your bank's government payments (SADAD) service using your iqama number. Then renew each family member's iqama in Absher under your dependents.
3. Allow time for the payment to show in Absher before you try to renew.

> Unpaid dependent fees also block other services. Jawazat has said dependents' fees must be paid before their iqama can be renewed or an exit re-entry visa issued for them.

## Late renewal fines

Renew no later than three days after the iqama expires. Jawazat's penalties for late renewal are:

- First time: SAR 500
- Second time: SAR 1,000
- Third time: deportation

## Family members outside Saudi Arabia

If a dependent or domestic worker is abroad when their iqama is due, it can still be renewed through the Ministry of Interior's platforms, but the renewal fee is doubled.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting a newborn. The fee applies to babies too, once they are added as dependents.
- Letting a family member's iqama lapse while they are on holiday. Renew before they travel, or pay the higher fee from abroad.
