## Before you start

- [ ] The new SIM is registered under your iqama number.
- [ ] The new SIM is in your phone and can receive SMS.
- [ ] Your passport details in Absher are up to date.

## Method 1: Absher app, without logging in

1. Open the Absher app. Do not log in.
2. Select **Digital Identity Management**.
3. Select **Change Mobile Number**.
4. Enter your iqama number and date of birth.
5. Enter the new number and confirm the code sent to it.
6. Complete the biometric (face) verification.

## Method 2: Nafath app

1. Install the Nafath app and open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Absher self-service services**, then **Update your digital identity (Absher)**.
3. Enter your iqama number, the new number and the code sent to it.
4. Complete the face verification. The change usually shows within minutes.

## Method 3: Absher self-service machine

Machines are in many malls, Jawazat offices and some airports.

1. Choose English and enter your iqama number.
2. Select **Update mobile**.
3. Scan your fingerprints when asked.
4. Enter the new number and the code sent to it.

## Common mistakes

- Using a family member's or company SIM. It must be in your name.
- Cancelling the old SIM before the update, if you plan to change it by logging in to Absher.
- Downloading look-alike apps. Use only the official Absher and Nafath apps.
