## What you need

- Your iqama number (new arrivals: border number).
- A Saudi mobile number registered on your iqama and not used on any other Absher account.
- An email address.
- Fingerprints registered with Jawazat (done when your iqama is issued).

## Step by step: register

1. Go to absher.sa and choose **Absher Individuals**, or open the Absher app.
2. Choose the option to create a new account.
3. Enter your iqama number, mobile number and email.
4. Choose a username and password.
5. Enter the code sent to your phone. Your account now exists but is not yet active.

## Step by step: activate

Choose one channel:

- **Self-service kiosk:** find one with the Absher kiosk locator, choose English, scan your fingerprint and enter the SMS code.
- **Bank channel:** several banks offer Absher activation through their app, online banking or ATM. Look for the Absher option.
- **Jawazat office:** an officer verifies you and activates the account.

## Step by step: Nafath

1. Download the Nafath app from your phone's app store.
2. Log in with your iqama number and Absher password.
3. Enter the code sent to the mobile number on your Absher account.
4. Set up the app on your own phone only.

When a website asks you to log in "via Nafath", open the Nafath app and tap the matching number shown on the website.

## Common mistakes

- Using a number registered under someone else's iqama. Activation will fail.
- Registering your new number on a relative's Absher account. It moves away from yours.
- Activating Nafath on someone else's phone. Nafath warns against this.
