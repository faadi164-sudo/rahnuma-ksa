## Why the Qiwa contract matters

Contract documentation on Qiwa is mandatory for private-sector employers. The documented contract is the official record of your job title, salary and terms. It is used for salary protection, job transfers, end-of-service calculations and any labor case.

## Step by step: view your contract

1. Go to **qiwa.sa** and choose **Individuals**, then log in with your iqama number through the national single sign-on (Absher or Nafath).
2. Open **Services** and select **Employment contracts**.
3. Choose **Your current contracts** to see the full contract.
4. Download a copy for your records.

## What to check

- [ ] **Name, iqama number and nationality** are correct.
- [ ] **Job title** matches what you agreed (and your iqama profession, if relevant to your work).
- [ ] **Basic salary, housing and transport allowances** match your offer letter.
- [ ] **Start date, contract length and end date.**
- [ ] **Working hours, weekly days off and annual leave.**
- [ ] **Notice period and probation.**
- [ ] Any **non-compete** or confidentiality clause.

## Step by step: respond to a new or changed contract

1. Open the contract request from your Qiwa notifications or the contracts page.
2. Read every field.
3. Choose **Approve** if it is right, **Reject** if it isn't, or **Request amendment** and say what needs changing.
4. Keep a screenshot of what you approved.

## Common mistakes

- Approving without reading because HR is in a hurry.
- Accepting a lower basic salary "for now". The documented wage is the record used in salary and end-of-service disputes.
- Ignoring the request. Respond to it on Qiwa, and if you have doubts, ask HR to explain in writing.
