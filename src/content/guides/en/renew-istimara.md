## Before you start

- [ ] **Fahas valid.** Book an inspection at a licensed periodic inspection centre if needed. For private cars over three years old it costs about SAR 115 including VAT.
- [ ] **Insurance active** and showing against the car in Absher.
- [ ] **Traffic fines paid.**
- [ ] **Istimara** expires within 180 days.

## Step by step

1. **Pay the fee.** In your bank app: government payments (SADAD) → traffic → vehicle registration renewal. Enter your iqama number and the car's sequence number (on the istimara card). SAR 100 a year.
2. **Log in to Absher** and go to traffic or vehicle services.
3. **Choose the car** and select **Renew vehicle registration**.
4. **Confirm.** The new expiry date appears in Absher. The digital istimara updates straight away.

## Book Fahas and check it is valid

An online appointment is mandatory for every periodic inspection (Fahas). Booking is free; you pay at the station or online when you book, depending on the station.

**Book an appointment**

1. Open the periodic technical inspection platform run by the Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization (SASO). The older Vehicles Safety Center address, vi.vsafety.sa, leads to it. There is also a Vehicles Safety app.
2. Choose **Book an appointment** and register if it is your first time.
3. Pick the vehicle type, region, city, station and time.
4. Enter the car's details from the istimara and confirm.
5. Bring the istimara and an ID on the day. The owner, a first-degree relative or an authorised driver can take the car.

**Fees for a private car**

| Item | SAR |
| --- | --- |
| Inspection | 115 including VAT |
| Re-inspection after a fail (two chances within 14 working days) | 37.95 including VAT |
| After 14 working days | Full fee again |

A pass is valid for one year. New private cars are exempt for their first three years.

**Check your Fahas validity**

- Look at the inspection sticker on the windscreen.
- In Absher, open your vehicles and the car's details.
- On the inspection platform, use the inspection status inquiry with the car's chassis number and the sequence number on the istimara.

## Common mistakes

- Paying the fee for the wrong number of years.
- Letting the insurance lapse a few days before renewal.
- Forgetting fines registered against the car by other drivers you've authorised.
