## Before you start

- [ ] **Fahas valid.** Book an inspection at a licensed periodic inspection centre if needed. For private cars over three years old it costs about SAR 115 including VAT.
- [ ] **Insurance active** and showing against the car in Absher.
- [ ] **Traffic fines paid.**
- [ ] **Istimara** expires within 180 days.

## Step by step

1. **Pay the fee.** In your bank app: government payments (SADAD) → traffic → vehicle registration renewal. Enter your iqama number and the car's sequence number (on the istimara card). SAR 100 a year.
2. **Log in to Absher** and go to traffic or vehicle services.
3. **Choose the car** and select **Renew vehicle registration**.
4. **Confirm.** The new expiry date appears in Absher. The digital istimara updates straight away.

## Common mistakes

- Paying the fee for the wrong number of years.
- Letting the insurance lapse a few days before renewal.
- Forgetting fines registered against the car by other drivers you've authorised.
