## Your options

- **Your bank's app.** International transfer to a bank account. Convenient if your salary is there.
- **Exchange-house apps and branches.** Often better rates for South Asian currencies, with cash pickup or bank deposit at home.
- **Digital wallets.** App-based transfers, usually funded from your Saudi bank account or card.

## Step by step (first transfer)

1. Open the app and add a beneficiary.
2. Enter the receiver's full name exactly as on their bank account or ID, their bank and branch, and their IBAN or account number, or choose cash pickup.
3. Enter the amount in riyals and check the rate, the fee and the **amount received**.
4. Confirm with the code sent to your phone.
5. Keep the transfer reference until the money arrives.

## How to compare the real cost

Total cost = transfer fee + exchange rate margin. A transfer with a SAR 0 fee but a weaker rate can cost you more than one with a small fee.

## For Pakistanis: Roshan Digital Account

A Roshan Digital Account is a Pakistani bank account you can open online from abroad without visiting a branch. You fund it by sending money from outside Pakistan.

## Avoid these mistakes

- A misspelled receiver name. The transfer can be rejected or held.
- Sending to a new beneficiary because of a message or call. Confirm by phone with your family first.
- Sharing app login codes with anyone, including people saying they're from the bank.
