## Types and fees

| Visa type | Base fee | Each extra month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single exit re-entry (one trip) | SAR 200 for up to 2 months | SAR 100 |
| Multiple exit re-entry (several trips) | SAR 500 for up to 3 months | SAR 200 |

Extra months can only be added within your iqama's remaining validity. The fee is paid through your bank's government payments (SADAD) service or in Absher, depending on who issues the visa.

## Conditions Jawazat checks

Before the visa can be issued:

- All traffic fines are paid.
- You do not already hold a valid, unused visa.
- You are inside Saudi Arabia when the visa is issued.
- Your passport is valid for at least 90 days.
- Your fingerprints are registered with Jawazat.
- Your iqama is valid, and for family members, their dependent fees are paid.

## Who issues the visa

- **Family members (dependents):** the person who sponsors them, on Absher Individuals.
- **Employees:** the employer, on Absher Business or Muqeem.
- **Employees issuing their own:** since the 2021 labor reforms, a worker can request their own exit re-entry visa on Absher. The request goes to the employer first. Absher shows the waiting period and conditions before you submit.

## Step by step: issuing a visa for a family member on Absher

1. Pay the visa fee against the family member's iqama number through your bank's government payments service.
2. Log in to Absher Individuals and open your dashboard.
3. Go to your dependents, choose the person, and select **Issue visa**.
4. Accept the terms.
5. Choose the visa type and set the duration (number of days or a "return before" date).
6. Confirm. The visa appears on the family member's record and can be printed.

## Step by step: requesting your own visa on Absher

1. Log in to Absher Individuals.
2. Go to **Services → Passports → Visa requests**.
3. Create a new request and choose **Exit re-entry visa (single)** or **(multiple)**.
4. Submit and wait for the period Absher shows. If your employer does not object, you can pay and issue the visa within the window that follows.

## Cancelling a visa

On Absher, open the services for sponsors, choose the person, and confirm cancellation. The fee is not refunded.

## Common mistakes

- Booking flights before the visa is issued. Issue the visa first.
- Choosing a visa that runs past your iqama's expiry. Renew the iqama first, then issue the visa.
- Forgetting unpaid traffic fines, which block issuance.
- Picking too short a duration. Extending from abroad costs double, so choose enough months up front.
