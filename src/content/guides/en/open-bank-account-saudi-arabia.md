## Before you start

- [ ] Valid iqama (some banks want at least 30 days left).
- [ ] Absher account activated.
- [ ] Nafath app working.
- [ ] Mobile number registered under your iqama.
- [ ] National address registered.

## Step by step

1. Download your chosen bank's official app, or open its website, and tap **Open account**.
2. Enter your iqama number and mobile number.
3. Approve the request in the Nafath app.
4. Fill in your employer, job and monthly income, and answer the residency and tax questions.
5. Create your login details and choose a debit (mada) card.
6. Confirm your national address for card delivery.
7. Give your IBAN to your employer's HR so your salary goes into this account.

## Common mistakes

- A mobile number not in your name. Online opening will fail.
- Opening accounts in several banks just to try them. Each can ask for updates and fees later.
- Leaving the ID expiry date out of date after renewing your iqama.
