## Before you start

- [ ] Valid iqama (some banks want at least 30 days left).
- [ ] Absher account activated.
- [ ] Nafath app working.
- [ ] Mobile number registered under your iqama.
- [ ] National address registered.

## Step by step

1. Download your chosen bank's official app, or open its website, and tap **Open account**.
2. Enter your iqama number and mobile number.
3. Approve the request in the Nafath app.
4. Fill in your employer, job and monthly income, and answer the residency and tax questions.
5. Create your login details and choose a debit (mada) card.
6. Confirm your national address for card delivery.
7. Give your IBAN to your employer's HR so your salary goes into this account.

## What should be free

The Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) requires banks and e-wallet providers to offer 25 core services free of charge. They include:

- Opening a current account or an e-wallet.
- Cash withdrawals and deposits, and cheque deposits.
- Using your mada card for shopping in Saudi Arabia, online purchases and payments across the GCC network.
- Account statements and customer identification certificates.

Banks also may not demand a minimum amount to open a current account, or close it because the balance is low. If a bank charges you for one of these, ask it to reverse the fee.

## After the account is open

- **Salary.** Give your IBAN to HR. Salaries are paid through the Wage Protection System (Mudad), which flags late payments, so the account your employer has on file must be correct.
- **Government fees.** Pay iqama renewals, dependent fees and exit re-entry visas in the government payments section of your app.
- **Bills.** Electricity, water and phone bills are paid through SADAD in the same app. Electricity is biller code 002 at most banks.
- **Money home.** Compare the amount your family receives, not just the fee. See our [guide to sending money home](/guides/send-money-home-from-saudi-arabia/).
- **Pakistanis.** A Roshan Digital Account lets you hold an account in Pakistan, opened online and funded from abroad.

## Common mistakes

- A mobile number not in your name. Online opening will fail.
- Opening accounts in several banks just to try them. Each can ask for updates and fees later.
- Leaving the ID expiry date out of date after renewing your iqama.
