## Which report do you need?

| Report | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| **Vehicle data report** | Selling or buying a car, insurance, a finance company |
| **Driving licence data report** | Proving your licence is valid and which categories it covers, for an employer or an embassy |
| **Traffic accidents report** | Showing your accident record, for example to an insurer or a new employer |
| **Traffic violations report** | Showing your recorded traffic violations |
| **Resident report** | Proving your residency details to a third party |

The list in Absher can change. Open the service to see every report available to you.

## Step by step: issue a report

1. Log in to Absher Individuals on the website or app.
2. Choose **My Services**, then **General Services**.
3. Open **Absher Reports** and read the conditions.
4. Choose **Request a report** and pick the report type. For a licence report, choose the licence type.
5. Pay the fee if Absher shows one.
6. Check the details and confirm.
7. Print or download the report.

Each report shows a reference number, version number, issue date and report type.

## Step by step: find a report you issued earlier

1. Go to **Absher Reports** again.
2. Choose **View previous reports**.
3. Select the report and open it. You can download it again while it is still valid.

## Step by step: check a report someone gave you

- **Scan the QR code** on the report with your phone camera. It should open an official Absher page that shows the same details.
- Or use **Inquiry about a report** in the Absher Reports service and enter the details printed on the report.

If the QR code opens anything other than an official Absher page, or the details don't match, don't rely on the report.

## Common mistakes

- Accepting a photo or screenshot instead of a report you can verify.
- Paying for the wrong report type. Read the list before you pay.
- Forgetting that a report is a snapshot: a car or licence record can change after the issue date.
