## Step by step: get the account in your name

1. **Through Ejar:** when the broker or landlord registers the lease, ask them to tick the option that transfers electricity and water to you.
2. **Or through Saudi Electricity:** log in to the app or website and use **meter declaration**, choosing **tenant**.
3. Note your **account number** from the app or first bill.

## Check your bill

- On the Saudi Electricity website, open **View and pay bill** and enter the account number (or the iqama number the meter is registered to). No login needed.
- Or check in the app after logging in.

## Pay your bill

1. Open your bank app → **SADAD bills**.
2. Add **Saudi Electricity Company** (may show as Saudi Energy; biller code 002 in most banks).
3. Enter your account number and pay. Partial payments are accepted.

## Common mistakes

- Moving in without moving the account, then inheriting the old tenant's arrears.
- Ignoring bills until arrears pass SAR 1,000.
- Not checking whose name the meter is in before signing the lease.
