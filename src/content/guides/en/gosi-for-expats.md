## What GOSI covers for expats

| Branch | Saudis | Expats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Occupational hazards (work injuries, work-related illness) | Yes | Yes, 2% paid by the employer |
| Pension | Yes | No |
| Unemployment insurance (SANED) | Yes | No |

## How to check your GOSI record

1. Open the GOSI app or website and log in with Nafath.
2. Check that your current employer is listed and that the start date is right.
3. Check the registered wage. It should match your basic salary plus housing allowance.

## Why the registered wage matters

- It is the wage used for work-injury compensation.
- If it's wrong, ask HR to correct it. Keep your contract and salary slips as proof.

## If you're injured at work

1. Get medical treatment and tell your employer straight away.
2. Make sure the employer reports the injury to GOSI.
3. Keep copies of medical reports and the employer's report.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming GOSI gives expats a pension or refund.
- Not checking the registered wage until there's a problem.
- Accepting salary deductions labelled GOSI without asking what they are.
