## Before you start

- [ ] Valid iqama and an active Absher account.
- [ ] Family registered in Absher.
- [ ] Salary certificate and proof of housing.
- [ ] If you're single, you must be at least 24.

## Two ways to recruit

| Route | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| By preferences | You set nationality, job and other requirements; licensed offices send CVs and prices |
| Known worker (Maarofa) | You name a specific worker you already know (limited nationalities) |

## Step by step

1. **Register on Musaned** with your iqama and verify your email.
2. **Choose a route** and compare the offers from licensed offices.
3. **Accept an offer** and pay inside Musaned (card or mada).
4. **Pay the visa fee:** SAR 2,000 per worker, through SADAD. People with disabilities are exempt.
5. **Sign the contract** electronically in Musaned.
6. **Receive the worker,** complete the medical check and iqama, and buy the required insurance.
7. **Pay the salary** every month through Musaned.

## If the contract ends

Workers with under two years in Saudi Arabia must leave on a final exit within 60 days. After two years, they can transfer to a new employer or take a final exit within that window.

## Common mistakes

- Using unlicensed brokers on social media. Hire only through Musaned.
- Paying salary in cash. It no longer meets the rules.
