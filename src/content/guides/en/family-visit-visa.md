## Before you start

- [ ] Your iqama is valid.
- [ ] Your Absher account and Nafath app are active.
- [ ] You have each visitor's passport details (valid for at least six months is the usual travel requirement).
- [ ] You know the relationship of each visitor to you.

## Step by step

1. Go to visa.mofa.gov.sa and switch to English.
2. Choose **Citizens and residents**, then **Resident**, then **Family visit visa application for residents**.
3. Log in as an individual with your iqama number and approve the request in the Nafath app.
4. Choose single or multiple entry and add each visitor: name as in the passport, nationality, relationship, passport number.
5. Review and submit. Note the application number.
6. Track the application on the platform. Processing usually takes a few working days.
7. When approved, download the approval and send it to your relatives.
8. Your relatives apply for the visa stamp through the Saudi embassy or its authorised visa centre in their country, pay the visa and insurance fees, and receive the visa.

## Fees

- **Government visa fee:** unified at SAR 300 by a 2019 Cabinet decision. The platform shows the current amount at payment.
- **Medical insurance:** mandatory for visitors, priced by age and length of stay.
- **Visa centre charges:** set by the visa centre in your relative's country.

## Common mistakes

- Spelling names differently from the passport.
- Choosing single entry when relatives may need to leave and come back.
- Letting the visitors overstay. Extend in time on Absher or make sure they leave before the permitted stay ends.
