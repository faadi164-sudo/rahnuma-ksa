## The new thresholds

| From | A Saudi employee counts as one full worker if paid at least | Counts as half a worker if paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Now | SAR 4,000 | — |
| 25 April 2027 | SAR 5,000 | SAR 4,000 to under 5,000 |
| 25 April 2028 | SAR 6,000 | SAR 5,000 to under 6,000 |

Some professions already have their own higher thresholds under separate Saudization decisions.

## Why it matters to expats

- Your employer's Nitaqat band decides whether it can get new work visas, renew work permits and transfer workers in. A company whose Saudi staff earn below the new thresholds may drop to a lower band.
- Companies in lower bands can face limits on visas and some services. If you plan to change jobs, check the new employer's Nitaqat status on Qiwa.
- The decision does not set a minimum salary for expats.
