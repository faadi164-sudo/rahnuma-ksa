## The new thresholds

| From | A Saudi employee counts as one full worker if paid at least | Counts as half a worker if paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Now | SAR 4,000 | — |
| 25 April 2027 | SAR 5,000 | SAR 4,000 to under 5,000 |
| 25 April 2028 | SAR 6,000 | SAR 5,000 to under 6,000 |

Some professions already have their own higher thresholds under separate Saudization decisions.

## A worked example

A company has 10 Saudi employees, each paid SAR 4,500 a month. Today they count as 10 full workers towards its Saudization rate. From 25 April 2027, each counts as half a worker, so the company is credited with only 5. Unless it raises those salaries to SAR 5,000 or hires more Saudis, its Saudization rate halves on that date, and it may move down a Nitaqat band.

## What the Nitaqat colours mean

Nitaqat sorts employers into bands by the share of Saudis they employ against the target for their sector and size. The lowest band, **red**, matters most to expat staff:

- A red employer can't get new work visas or renew work permits.
- Its expat workers can transfer to another employer **without the current employer's consent**.

You can check an employer's band through the Ministry of Human Resources inquiry service or on Qiwa.

## Why it matters to expats

- Your employer's Nitaqat band decides whether it can get new work visas, renew work permits and transfer workers in. A company whose Saudi staff earn below the new thresholds may drop to a lower band.
- Companies in lower bands can face limits on visas and some services. If you plan to change jobs, check the new employer's Nitaqat status on Qiwa.
- The decision does not set a minimum salary for expats.

## Other Saudization dates to watch

| Decision | Rule | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procurement jobs | 70% Saudis in 12 procurement jobs | 31 May 2026 |
| Gyms and sports centres | 15% Saudis in 12 coaching and supervisory jobs | 18 November 2026 |
| Project management jobs | 70% Saudis (up from 40%) in firms with 3 or more such staff | 14 February 2027 |
| Nitaqat salary threshold | SAR 5,000 for a Saudi to count fully | 25 April 2027 |
| Nitaqat salary threshold | SAR 6,000 | 25 April 2028 |

## What to do now

1. Check your employer's Nitaqat band on Qiwa, and check it again after 25 April 2027.
2. Make sure your own contract is documented on Qiwa. A documented contract is one of the conditions for moving jobs.
3. If your employer drops to red, read our guide to changing jobs without your employer's consent.
