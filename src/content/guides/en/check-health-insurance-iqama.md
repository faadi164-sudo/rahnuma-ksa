## Method 1: Council of Health Insurance (CHI)

1. Open the CHI website and find the insurance information inquiry.
2. Enter your iqama number (or border number, or passport number for visitors).
3. Type the verification code and submit.
4. You'll see the insurer, policy number, class and expiry date.

## Method 2: Insurance Authority (IA)

1. Open the Insurance Authority's **Check Health Insurance** service.
2. Enter your 10-digit iqama number.
3. Tick the consent box and press **Search**.
4. Read your status, validity and insurer.

## Which number to use

| You are | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Resident with an iqama | Iqama number |
| New arrival without an iqama yet | Border number |
| On a visit, Umrah or Hajj visa | Passport number |

## Who your employer must insure

Private-sector employers must insure all their employees, Saudi and non-Saudi, and the family members covered by the system, through a licensed insurer.

| Person | Who insures them |
| --- | --- |
| You | Your employer |
| Your wife | Your employer, unless she works and her own employer insures her |
| Sons | Your employer, up to age 25 |
| Daughters | Your employer, while unmarried and not working |
| Parents, brothers, sisters you sponsor | You buy a policy yourself |

If both parents work in the private sector, the children are covered under the father's policy unless the mother's employer adds them. Each person can only have one health insurance policy.

An employer that doesn't insure covered staff and family, or stops paying, must pay the premiums owed plus a fine of up to a year's premium for each person. The Council of Health Insurance follows up on employer compliance.

## Visitors on a visit visa

Visitors need their own cover. Every visit visa, and every extension, comes with medical insurance that covers emergencies only, up to SAR 100,000 per visitor, starting from the day they enter Saudi Arabia. It isn't a full health policy, so routine treatment is paid separately. To check a visitor's cover, use the Insurance Authority's **Visit Visa Travel Insurance Inquiry** with the passport number used for the visa. See our [guide to visit visa medical insurance](/guides/visit-visa-medical-insurance/).

## If something is wrong

- Expired or missing: tell HR straight away. An iqama can't be renewed without valid insurance.
- Wrong class or details: ask HR to correct it with the insurer.
- Family not insured: ask HR to add your wife and children. Employers are required to cover them, and their iqamas can't be issued or renewed without valid insurance.
- Changed jobs: check again after your transfer. Your new employer's policy should replace the old one.
