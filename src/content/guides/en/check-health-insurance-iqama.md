## Method 1: Council of Health Insurance (CHI)

1. Open the CHI website and find the insurance information inquiry.
2. Enter your iqama number (or border number, or passport number for visitors).
3. Type the verification code and submit.
4. You'll see the insurer, policy number, class and expiry date.

## Method 2: Insurance Authority (IA)

1. Open the Insurance Authority's **Check Health Insurance** service.
2. Enter your 10-digit iqama number.
3. Tick the consent box and press **Search**.
4. Read your status, validity and insurer.

## Which number to use

| You are | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Resident with an iqama | Iqama number |
| New arrival without an iqama yet | Border number |
| On a visit, Umrah or Hajj visa | Passport number |

## If something is wrong

- Expired or missing: tell HR straight away. An iqama can't be renewed without valid insurance.
- Wrong class or details: ask HR to correct it with the insurer.
