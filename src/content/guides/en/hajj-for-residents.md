## Who can apply

- Expats with a valid iqama (and Saudi citizens).
- Aged at least 15 (the 2026 rule), and medically fit.
- Priority for first-time pilgrims. In 2026, those who had performed Hajj before could register in a second phase from 18 April.

## Step by step

1. **Watch for the announcement.** For 1447 (2026), registration opened on 23 February (early Ramadan).
2. **Create a Nusuk account** on the app or nusuk.sa and enter your details yourself.
3. **Check eligibility.** Nusuk confirms whether you can book.
4. **Choose a package.** Packages come only from licensed Hajj companies listed in Nusuk. They differ by camp location, tent type and transport.
5. **Pay through SADAD** before the deadline shown.
6. **Get your Hajj permit** in Nusuk and complete any required vaccinations.

## Common mistakes

- Booking with unlicensed "Hajj groups" on social media. Book only through Nusuk.
- Missing the payment deadline. Unpaid bookings are released.
- Leaving vaccinations until the last week.
