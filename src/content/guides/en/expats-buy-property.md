## Who can buy and where

| Buyer | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Non-Saudi residents | Approved zones, plus one home for personal use outside them (not in Makkah or Madinah); one home per family |
| Non-residents | Approved zones |
| Muslims (resident or not) | Also within set zones in Makkah and Madinah |
| Non-Muslims | Not in Makkah or Madinah |

## Costs

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Real estate transaction tax (ZATCA) | 5% (often paid by the seller, can be shifted by contract) |
| Extra fee on non-Saudi purchases in Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah | 2% (law allows up to 5%) |
| Broker's commission | Up to 2.5% |
| VAT | 15% on most new property sales, generally not on resale homes |

## Step by step

1. **Get ready:** valid iqama, Nafath, a Saudi bank account in your name and a Saudi mobile number linked to your digital identity.
2. **Use a licensed broker** and agree the property.
3. **Apply on Saudi Properties** (REGA's platform): log in with Nafath, choose the property, fill in the form and attach documents.
4. **Pay fees** and submit. Eligibility is checked automatically.
5. **Register the property** in the Real Estate Registry once approved. Unregistered purchases have no legal effect.

## Common mistakes

- Paying a deposit before checking the property is in an approved zone.
- Giving incomplete or misleading information. Fines can reach SAR 10 million.
- Forgetting the 5% transaction tax and the extra 2% fee when budgeting.
