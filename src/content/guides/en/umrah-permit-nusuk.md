## Step by step: book an Umrah permit

1. Download the **Nusuk** app.
2. Register with your iqama number, mobile number and email, and verify with the code you receive.
3. Open the Umrah permit service.
4. Choose a date and an available time slot.
5. Add companions: family members on your record can be added to the same booking.
6. Confirm. Each person gets a permit with a QR code in the app.
7. On the day, carry your iqama and show the QR code at the Grand Mosque.

## Step by step: book a Rawdah permit (Madinah)

1. In Nusuk, open the Rawdah permit service.
2. Choose a date and time. Men and women have separate slots.
3. Confirm and keep the QR code ready.

## Tips

- Screenshot your permit in case your connection drops.
- If plans change, cancel the permit in the app so the slot goes to someone else.
- Book transport and a hotel only after you have the permit.

## Common mistakes

- Arriving outside your booked time slot.
- Travelling with an expired iqama. Renew it first.
- Assuming the Rawdah is included in the Umrah permit. It is a separate booking.
