> **Dates are expected only.** Saudi Arabia confirms the start of Ramadan and Eid after the moon sighting. The dates below come from the Umm al-Qura calendar.

## Expected dates for 2027

| Event | Expected date |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Ramadan 1448 | Monday 8 February 2027 |
| Founding Day (public holiday, falls in Ramadan) | Monday 22 February 2027 |
| Eid al-Fitr (1 Shawwal 1448) | Tuesday 9 March 2027 |

## Working hours

| Who | Ramadan hours |
| --- | --- |
| Muslim employees, private sector | At most 6 hours a day or 36 hours a week (Article 98) |
| Non-Muslim employees, private sector | The law doesn't require shorter hours; many employers give them anyway |
| Government employees | Set each year by official announcement |

- Hours worked beyond the Ramadan limit count as **overtime**. Our [overtime calculator](/tools/overtime-calculator/) has a 6-hour option for Ramadan.
- Prayer, meal and rest breaks are not counted as working hours.

## Eating, drinking and smoking in public

The Ministry of Interior has repeatedly told non-Muslim residents not to eat, drink or smoke in public places, including streets and workplaces, during fasting hours, and has warned that violators can lose their jobs and be deported. In practice:

- Eat and drink at home, in a private office or in a pantry set aside by your employer.
- Don't chew gum, drink water or smoke in your car with the windows down, in malls or on the street before sunset.
- Children who aren't fasting can eat at home and school as usual; just keep it out of public view.

## Umrah and Taraweeh

- Residents need an Umrah permit on **Nusuk** for a specific date and time, and Ramadan slots fill quickly, especially in the last ten nights. See our [Umrah permit guide](/guides/umrah-permit-nusuk/).
- Praying in the Rawdah in Madinah needs a separate Nusuk permit.

## Zakat al-Fitr

- Muslims give **one sa' of staple food (about 3 kg)**, such as rice, for themselves and each family member.
- Give it before the Eid prayer. Many people buy rice from shops near mosques or give through official charity platforms.

## Eid al-Fitr holiday

- Private-sector employees get **4 paid days** for Eid al-Fitr, starting the day after 29 Ramadan. Your contract can give more, not fewer.
- If you work on an Eid holiday, it counts as overtime. See our [2027 public holidays guide](/guides/saudi-public-holidays-2027/).

## Planning Hajj 1448?

In 2026, registration for domestic pilgrims opened on 6 Ramadan, and package booking on 15 Ramadan. Expect the 1448 registration around Ramadan too, and watch Nusuk. See our [Hajj for residents guide](/guides/hajj-for-residents/).

## Common mistakes

- Booking Umrah transport and hotels before getting the Nusuk permit.
- Assuming your employer must give non-Muslim staff shorter hours. Check the company announcement.
- Drinking water while driving in traffic during fasting hours.
