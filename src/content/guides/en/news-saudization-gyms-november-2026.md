## What changes

- **Who:** private-sector men's and women's sports centres and gyms with four or more employees.
- **What:** at least 15% of workers in 12 designated jobs must be Saudi nationals.
- **When:** from 18 November 2026.
- **Jobs covered include:** sports coach, professional football coach, sports supervisor, personal trainer and professional athletics coach. The full list is in the ministry's procedural guide.

## What it means for expat trainers and coaches

- Expats can still work in these jobs, but each covered gym must meet the 15% Saudi share.
- Employers who don't comply face penalties, so some may reduce expat hiring in these roles.
- If you work in one of these jobs, check your employer's position on Qiwa and keep your contract documented. If your job ends, see our guides on changing jobs and on end-of-service benefit.

## Support for employers

The ministry offers help with recruitment, training, qualification, employment and keeping staff. Gyms covered by the decision also get priority access to its localization support programmes and to the Human Resources Development Fund (Hadaf). The ministry says legal penalties will apply to establishments that don't comply.

## How it fits with Nitaqat

This decision sets a share for specific jobs. Separately, every employer has an overall Nitaqat band based on how many Saudis it employs. If a gym falls into the **red** band, it can't get new work visas or renew work permits, and its expat staff can move to another employer **without its consent**. Check your employer's band on Qiwa.

## Your options as an expat coach or trainer

- **Stay:** the rule sets a 15% Saudi share in these jobs, so expats can still fill the rest.
- **Move to another gym:** job transfers through Qiwa need, among other conditions, a year with your current employer, a documented contract and notice to the employer. Some cases, such as unpaid salaries for three months or a red-band employer, allow a transfer without consent.
- **Change role:** if you move into a job outside the 12, your employer can request a profession change on Qiwa.
- **Leave:** if your job ends, check your end-of-service benefit with our calculator.
