## What changes

- **Who:** private-sector men's and women's sports centres and gyms with four or more employees.
- **What:** at least 15% of workers in 12 designated jobs must be Saudi nationals.
- **When:** from 18 November 2026.
- **Jobs covered include:** sports coach, professional football coach, sports supervisor, personal trainer and professional athletics coach. The full list is in the ministry's procedural guide.

## What it means for expat trainers and coaches

- Expats can still work in these jobs, but each covered gym must meet the 15% Saudi share.
- Employers who don't comply face penalties, so some may reduce expat hiring in these roles.
- If you work in one of these jobs, check your employer's position on Qiwa and keep your contract documented. If your job ends, see our guides on changing jobs and on end-of-service benefit.

## Support for employers

The ministry offers Saudization support programmes, including recruitment, training and wage support through the Human Resources Development Fund (Hadaf).
