## Which one to use

| Certificate | Where | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qiwa salary certificate | qiwa.sa (individual account) | Banks, embassies, landlords, any entity that can verify online |
| GOSI wage certificate | GOSI website or app | Proof that you are registered and your wage is reported |
| Employer letter | Your HR department | When an entity asks for letterhead, a signature and a stamp |

## Step by step: Qiwa salary certificate

1. Log in to **qiwa.sa** as an individual.
2. Go to **Services** and choose **Salary certificates**.
3. Start a new certificate.
4. Choose the entity you are giving it to (bank, embassy, company, university and so on). If it isn't listed, choose **Other** and type the name.
5. Confirm and issue it.
6. To download or print it later, open **Salary certificates** again, find it in the list and download the PDF.

## Step by step: GOSI wage certificate

1. Log in to the GOSI website or app with your national single sign-on.
2. Open the certificates section.
3. Choose the wage certificate and download the PDF.

## Letter format for HR (English)

> **Salary Certificate**
>
> Date: [date]
>
> To: [bank, embassy or organisation name]
>
> This is to certify that Mr./Ms. [full name], [nationality], holder of iqama number [number] and passport number [number], has been employed with [company name] as [job title] since [joining date].
>
> His/her current monthly salary is SAR [total], made up of basic salary SAR [amount], housing allowance SAR [amount] and transport allowance SAR [amount].
>
> This certificate is issued at the employee's request without any responsibility on the company's part.
>
> [Name, title, signature and company stamp]

## Common mistakes

- Giving a bank a letter whose salary doesn't match your Qiwa contract or GOSI record. Banks check.
- Choosing the wrong receiving entity on Qiwa, so the certificate is addressed to the wrong place.
- Forgetting that certificates have a short shelf life for banks and embassies; issue a fresh one when you apply.
