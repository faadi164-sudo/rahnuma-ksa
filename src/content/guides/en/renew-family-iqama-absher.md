## Before you start

- [ ] Your own iqama is valid.
- [ ] Each family member's passport is valid and its details are up to date in Absher.
- [ ] Traffic fines on your record are paid.
- [ ] You have decided the renewal period (3, 6, 9 or 12 months).

## Step 1: pay the fees

1. Open your bank app or online banking and go to government payments (SADAD).
2. Choose the Passports (Jawazat) biller and the iqama renewal service for dependents.
3. Enter the family member's iqama number and pay the iqama fee and the dependent fee for the chosen period.
4. Repeat for each family member.

## Step 2: renew in Absher

1. Log in to Absher Individuals.
2. Open **Family Members Services** (for a maid or driver: **Domestic Workers Services**).
3. Select **Resident Services**.
4. Choose the family member.
5. Select **Renew Iqama**, read the terms and confirm.
6. The new expiry date appears on their record. The digital iqama in the Absher app updates too.

## If a family member is abroad

- You must be inside Saudi Arabia.
- The renewal fee is double for someone outside the Kingdom.
- Their exit re-entry visa can also be extended online. See our guide on extending an exit re-entry visa from abroad.

## Common mistakes

- Paying for a different number of months than you choose in Absher.
- An outdated passport number in Absher. Update the passport first.
- Waiting until the last day. The fine for late renewal starts three days after expiry.
