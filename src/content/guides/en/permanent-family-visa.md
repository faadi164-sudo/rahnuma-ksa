## Before you start

- [ ] Your iqama is valid (at least three months left is commonly required).
- [ ] Your profession is eligible for family sponsorship. Absher will say so if it isn't.
- [ ] Marriage certificate and children's birth certificates are attested in your home country and by the Saudi embassy or MOFA, then translated into Arabic.
- [ ] Family passports have at least six months' validity.
- [ ] A salary certificate or GOSI record, if HR asks for it.

## Who you can sponsor

- Your wife.
- Your children: sons usually up to a set age, and unmarried daughters.
- Not parents, brothers or sisters. They come on visit visas.

## Step by step

1. **Apply in Absher.** Log in, go to **My Services → Expatriates Services**, and choose the family visa application. Add each family member.
2. **Print the form and give it to HR.** The company signs and stamps it and gets it attested by the Chamber of Commerce.
3. **Pay the visa fee.** SAR 2,000 through your bank's government payments service (SADAD).
4. **Wait for approval.** Check the status in Absher. You get a visa number.
5. **Stamping.** Your family submits the visa number, passports and documents at the Saudi visa centre in your home country.
6. **Travel.** They enter Saudi Arabia on the stamped visa.
7. **Iqamas.** After arrival: medical tests, health insurance, then pay the iqama fee and the SAR 400 monthly dependent fee per person, and issue the iqamas in Absher.

## Common mistakes

- Unattested or untranslated marriage and birth certificates. This is the most common reason for delays at stamping.
- Confusing it with the family visit visa. A visit visa does not give the family an iqama.
- Not budgeting the dependent fee: SAR 400 a month for each family member.
