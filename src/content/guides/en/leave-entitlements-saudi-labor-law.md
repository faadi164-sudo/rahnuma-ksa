These are the minimum paid leave rights in the Saudi Labor Law for private-sector workers, including expats. Your contract can give more but not less. The 2025 amendments, in force since 19 February 2025, increased maternity leave and added paternity and sibling bereavement leave.

## All leave at a glance

| Leave | How much | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | 21 days a year; 30 days after 5 consecutive years | Full |
| Eid al-Fitr | 4 days, from the day after 29 Ramadan | Full |
| Eid al-Adha | 4 days, from the day of Arafat | Full |
| Founding Day and National Day | 1 day each (22 February, 23 September) | Full |
| Sick leave | 30 days, then 60 days, then 30 days in a year | Full, then three-quarters, then none |
| Maternity leave | 12 weeks | Full |
| Nursing hour | 1 hour a day for 24 months after the birth | Full |
| Paternity leave | 3 days, within 7 days of the birth | Full |
| Your marriage | 5 days | Full |
| Death of a spouse, parent or child | 5 days | Full |
| Death of a brother or sister | 3 days | Full |
| Hajj leave | 10 to 15 days including Eid al-Adha, once | Full |
| Widow's leave (iddah) | Muslim woman: 4 months and 10 days; non-Muslim woman: 15 days | Full |
| Unpaid leave | As agreed with your employer | None |

## Sick leave: three stages

Article 117 gives a worker who falls ill, within one year:

1. **30 days on full pay.**
2. **The next 60 days on three-quarters pay.**
3. **The next 30 days without pay.**

The year is counted from your first sick leave, not from January or your joining date. The days add up whether you are off in one block or several short absences, and each stage starts where the last one ended.

**Example:** on a monthly wage of SAR 6,000, the first 30 sick days are paid in full, the next 60 days are paid at SAR 4,500 a month, and the last 30 days are unpaid.

Keep the sick leave report from your doctor or hospital and send it to HR as your employer's policy requires.

## Maternity leave and pregnancy

- **12 weeks on full pay.** Six weeks must be taken after the birth. You can spread the other six weeks as you choose, starting up to four weeks before the expected date of delivery.
- **Extra unpaid month.** You can extend maternity leave once by one unpaid month, or two months if the baby is born sick or with a disability.
- **Nursing hour.** When you return, you get one hour a day for nursing for 24 months.
- **Protection.** Your employer may not dismiss you while you are pregnant, on maternity leave, or ill because of either.

## Paternity, marriage and bereavement leave

- **Paternity:** fathers get 3 paid days, which must be taken within seven days of the birth.
- **Marriage:** 5 paid days for your own marriage.
- **Death of a spouse, parent or child:** 5 paid days.
- **Death of a brother or sister:** 3 paid days, added in 2025.
- **Widow's leave (iddah):** a Muslim woman whose husband dies gets at least 4 months and 10 days on full pay; a non-Muslim woman gets 15 days. She may not work for anyone else during this time, and the employer can ask for documents.

## Hajj leave

- **10 to 15 days on full pay,** including the Eid al-Adha holiday.
- **Once** during your service, and only if you haven't performed Hajj before.
- **After at least two consecutive years** with the same employer.
- Your employer can decide how many staff get Hajj leave each year, based on work needs, so apply early.

For registration and packages, see our [Hajj for residents guide](/guides/hajj-for-residents/).

## Unpaid leave

You can take unpaid leave if your employer agrees, for a period you agree between you. If unpaid leave goes beyond 20 days, your contract is suspended for the extra days unless you and your employer agree otherwise.

## Annual leave reminders

- Annual leave is 21 days, rising to 30 days after five consecutive years with the same employer, and it is paid in advance.
- Unused annual leave is paid in cash when you leave your job.
- Use our [annual leave calculator](/tools/annual-leave-calculator/) to work out your leave pay and the value of unused days.

## If your employer refuses leave you're entitled to

1. Ask in writing, quoting the type of leave, and keep a copy.
2. Raise it with HR or your manager.
3. If it isn't resolved, file a complaint through the Ministry's friendly settlement service. See our [guide to labor complaints](/guides/labor-complaint-friendly-settlement/).
