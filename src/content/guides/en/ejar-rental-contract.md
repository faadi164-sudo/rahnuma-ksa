## Step by step: accepting a contract

1. Give the landlord or broker your iqama number and the mobile number registered in your name.
2. Wait for the SMS from Ejar with your link.
3. Register as a tenant, verifying with the codes sent to you.
4. Open the contract and check every detail (see the list below).
5. Accept it, or reject it and ask for corrections.
6. Download a copy of the contract from your Ejar account.

## Check before you accept

- [ ] Your name and iqama number are correct.
- [ ] The unit's address and number match the flat you're renting.
- [ ] Rent amount, payment schedule and number of payments.
- [ ] Contract start date and length.
- [ ] Who pays electricity and water, and any service charges.

## Renewal and leaving

- Contracts renew automatically unless one party gives notice at least 60 days before the end.
- To leave, give notice within that window and agree the handover date with the landlord.
- Get the termination recorded in Ejar so the unit isn't still linked to you.

## Common mistakes

- Paying rent or a deposit before the contract is in Ejar.
- Accepting without reading the payment schedule.
- Ignoring the 60-day objection window on details you don't agree with.
