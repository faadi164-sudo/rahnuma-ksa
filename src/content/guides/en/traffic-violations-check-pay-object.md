## Step by step: check your violations

1. Log in to Absher Individuals.
2. Go to **My Services → Traffic Services → Traffic violations**.
3. You'll see each violation with its date, place, type and amount. Vehicles registered to you and violations by authorised drivers can appear too.

## Step by step: pay

- **Absher:** select the violation and pay with the method offered.
- **Efaa:** log in at efaa.sa and pay your active violations.
- **Bank app:** government payments (SADAD) → traffic violations → enter your iqama number.

> Pay within 30 days of notification to get the 25% discount.

## Step by step: object

1. In Absher, open the traffic violation objection service.
2. Choose the violation (within 30 days of it being recorded).
3. Explain clearly why it is wrong and attach any evidence.
4. Submit and follow the result in Absher.

## Step by step: get more time

1. In Absher, go to **My Services → Traffic Services → Extend traffic fine payment deadline**.
2. Choose the violation and submit. Absher shows the new deadline.

> Sources differ on when you can ask and how much extra time you get (60 or 90 days). Absher shows what applies to your violation, so go by what it says.

If you have many violations or a large total, Absher also offers payment in instalments.

## Common mistakes

- Ignoring a fine until you need an exit re-entry visa. Unpaid fines block it.
- Missing the 30-day objection window.
- Paying through unofficial apps or agents. Use Absher, Efaa or your bank only.
