## Route 1: health or education job (Ajeer dependents service)

**Who qualifies**

- The dependent is the main worker's spouse, a spouse's relative, or under the worker's legal guardianship.
- Aged 18 or over.
- Both the dependent and the main worker hold valid iqamas.
- She has the professional accreditation the job needs (for example from the health specialties commission or the education authorities).

**The employer must** be registered with the Ministry, have an active commercial registration, have no restrictions, and not be in the red Nitaqat band.

**Steps (done by the employer)**

1. Log in to the Ajeer platform.
2. Choose the **Ajeer dependents service**.
3. Fill in and submit the contract.
4. Issue the dependent's permit. The service is free.

## Route 2: any other job (transfer of sponsorship)

1. Get a job offer from an employer that can hire expats.
2. The employer requests her transfer to its sponsorship and issues a work permit.
3. Once moved, she has her own work iqama and is no longer your dependent.

## Common mistakes

- Starting work before the permit is issued.
- Assuming any sector works through Ajeer. The dependents service covers health and education.
- Forgetting accreditation, which health and teaching jobs need anyway.
