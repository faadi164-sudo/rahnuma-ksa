## Before you start

- [ ] Your licence expires within 180 days (or has already expired).
- [ ] Your iqama is valid.
- [ ] No unpaid traffic fines on your record.

## Step by step

1. **Medical check.** Do the driving medical at an Efada-approved centre. Wait until it shows in Absher (usually within 24–48 hours).
2. **Pay fines.** Check and pay any traffic violations in Absher or Efaa.
3. **Pay the renewal fee.** In your bank app: government payments (SADAD) → traffic → driving licence renewal. Choose 2, 5 or 10 years.
4. **Renew in Absher.** Go to **Services → Traffic → Renew Driving License**, choose the period you paid for and confirm.
5. **Get the licence.** The digital licence updates in Absher. Request delivery of a printed card to your national address if you want one.

## Fees

| Period | Fee (SAR) |
| --- | --- |
| 2 years | 80 |
| 5 years | 200 |
| 10 years | 400 |
| Late fee, if already expired | 100 for each year expired |

## Common mistakes

- Paying for a different number of years than you select in Absher.
- Trying to renew before the medical result has linked to Absher.
- Leaving it until after expiry. The late fee and the fine for driving on an expired licence add up quickly.
