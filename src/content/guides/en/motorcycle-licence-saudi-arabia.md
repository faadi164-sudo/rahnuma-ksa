## Before you start

- [ ] **Valid iqama** (regular residency is required for non-Saudis).
- [ ] **Absher account** active.
- [ ] **Medical check** at an approved clinic.
- [ ] **Unpaid traffic fines** cleared.
- [ ] **Foreign licence** with a motorcycle category, if you hope to convert rather than train.

## Step by step

1. **Check whether you can convert.** If your home licence includes motorcycles and your nationality is eligible for conversion, ask the driving school or Muroor whether you can take the conversion route. See our guide to converting a foreign licence.
2. **Book a driving school** that offers motorcycle training through Absher or the school's own system.
3. **Do the medical check** and upload or present the result.
4. **Complete the training** the school sets for you.
5. **Pass the theory and practical tests.**
6. **Pay the licence fee** through your bank's government payments: SAR 20 for each year of validity.
7. **Issue the licence.** Track it in Absher.

## Common mistakes

- Riding on a car licence and getting fined or uninsured after an accident.
- Paying a school before confirming it offers motorcycle courses at your branch.
- Forgetting a helmet. Ride with proper safety gear at all times.
