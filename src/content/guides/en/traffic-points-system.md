## Points per violation

| Violation | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs | 24 |
| Drifting | 24 |
| Running a red light | 12 |
| Driving against the direction of traffic | 12 |
| Weaving dangerously through traffic | 8 |
| Ignoring a traffic officer's hand signal | 8 |
| Driving without working brakes or lights | 8 |
| Not stopping fully at a STOP sign | 6 |
| Not giving way | 6 |
| Speeding more than 25 km/h over the limit | 6 |
| Not giving way in a roundabout | 6 |
| Overtaking where it's prohibited | 6 |
| Stopping on railway tracks | 6 |
| Speeding up to 25 km/h over the limit | 4 |
| Driving in a lane not meant for your vehicle | 4 |
| Overtaking a school bus while children get on or off | 4 |
| Uncovered load | 4 |
| Unauthorised changes to the vehicle body | 4 |
| Not wearing a seatbelt | 2 |
| Using a handheld phone while driving | 2 |
| No helmet on a motorbike | 2 |

## When your licence is suspended (24 points in a Hijri year)

| Time reaching 24 points | Penalty |
| --- | --- |
| First | Licence suspended 3 months |
| Second | Licence suspended 6 months |
| Third | Licence suspended 1 year |
| Fourth | Licence revoked |

## How to check your record

1. Log in to Absher and open traffic services.
2. Check your violations and their details.

## Common mistakes

- Thinking "small" violations don't matter. Speeding and phone use add up fast.
- Ignoring points because the fine was paid.
- Driving during a suspension.
