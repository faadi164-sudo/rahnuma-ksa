## Decisions to watch

| Sector or jobs | Rate | Applies to | From | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 procurement jobs (procurement manager, procurement specialist, warehouse manager and others) | 70% | Private sector | 31 May 2026 | In force |
| 12 gym and sports-centre jobs (coaches, personal trainers, supervisors) | 15% | Gyms with 4+ staff | 18 Nov 2026 | Starts soon |
| Project management director, engineer and specialist | 70% (up from 40%) | Firms with 3+ such workers | 14 Feb 2027 | Announced |
| Nitaqat: salary for a Saudi to count as one full worker | SAR 5,000 (SAR 6,000 from 2028) | All Nitaqat firms | 25 Apr 2027 | Announced |

## What it means for expats

- **Iqama and work permit renewal:** a firm in a low Nitaqat band can be blocked from renewing work permits. Ask HR about your firm's band in Qiwa.
- **Job changes:** moving into a Saudized title may be refused if the new employer is below the rate.
- **New hiring:** employers may stop recruiting expats into these titles before the date.

## How to check if you're affected

1. Log in to Qiwa (individuals) and check the profession on your contract.
2. Compare it with the professions in the ministry's procedural guide for each decision.
3. If it matches, talk to HR well before your iqama or contract renewal date.
