## Step by step: the full list (with a registered number)

1. Open the CST Mutasil individuals service.
2. Switch the language to English.
3. Enter your iqama number and date of birth.
4. When asked if you have a mobile number, choose **Yes** and enter one of your numbers.
5. Click **Inquire** and enter the code sent by SMS.
6. You will see each SIM: the network, number and type of subscription.

## Step by step: a quick count (no registered number)

1. Follow steps 1 to 3 above.
2. Choose **No** when asked about a mobile number.
3. Click **Inquire**. You will see how many SIMs each network has registered under your iqama, but not the numbers.

## If you find a number you don't recognise

1. Note the network and number.
2. Contact the network's customer service and ask them to cancel it, or file a complaint through the CST website.
3. Check again after a few days to confirm it has gone.

## Good habits

- Check before your final exit, and after giving your iqama copy to any shop or agent.
- Cancel old lines you no longer use instead of letting them lapse with unpaid bills.
