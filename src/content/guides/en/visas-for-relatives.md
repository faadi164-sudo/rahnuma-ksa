## Compare the options

| Visa | Who can use it | Applied by | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family visit visa | Your relatives (parents, spouse, children, siblings and others allowed) | You, on the MOFA platform | Longer family stays |
| Tourist e-visa | Citizens of ~66 eligible countries; holders of a used US, UK or Schengen visa; GCC residents | The traveller, online | Short visits, tourism, Umrah |
| Package visa (pilot) | Citizens of Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Egypt, Indonesia, Jordan and Mexico | Through a tourist package booking | Short tourist trips |
| Umrah visa | Pilgrims | Through an Umrah provider or Nusuk | Umrah-focused trips |

## Tourist e-visa: key points

- Eligible nationalities apply online before travelling.
- Other nationalities qualify if they hold a valid US, UK or Schengen tourist or business visa that has been used at least once, or are permanent residents there.
- GCC residents qualify if their residence permit is valid for at least three months after the entry date.
- The visa allows tourism, family visits and Umrah, but not Hajj.

## Package visa pilot (Pakistan and India included)

The visa is issued as part of a tourist package booking. ARY News reported the visa and travel health insurance at SAR 402.21, with extra charges for additional days.

## Which to choose

1. **Pakistani or Indian relatives with no US, UK, Schengen or GCC visa:** family visit visa.
2. **Relatives who hold a used US, UK or Schengen visa, or live in the GCC:** tourist e-visa, the fastest option.
3. **Mainly for Umrah:** Umrah visa or a package that includes it.

## Common mistakes

- Applying for an e-visa with a US, UK or Schengen visa that has never been used.
- Using a tourist visa to work. That isn't allowed.
- Overstaying. Check the stay allowed on each visa.
