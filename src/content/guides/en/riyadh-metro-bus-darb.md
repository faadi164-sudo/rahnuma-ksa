## The six lines

| Line | Main road or area | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Blue | Olaya Street, Al Batha and Al Hayer | 38 km |
| 2 Red | King Abdullah Road | 25.3 km |
| 3 Orange | Madinah Road | 40.7 km |
| 4 Yellow | King Khalid International Airport Road (airport to KAFD) | 29.5 km |
| 5 Green | King Abdulaziz Road | 12.9 km |
| 6 Purple | Abdulrahman bin Awf and Sheikh Hassan bin Hussein roads | 29.7 km |

All six lines have been running since January 2025. Four main stations connect several lines; King Abdullah Financial District (KAFD), for example, is where the Yellow and Purple lines meet.

## Fares

| Pass | Standard (SAR) | First class (SAR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 hours | 4 | 10 |
| 3 days | 20 | 50 |
| 7 days | 40 | 100 |
| 30 days | 140 | 350 |

Every pass gives unlimited metro and bus rides while it is valid. An annual standard pass was introduced in 2026 for SAR 1,260. First class does not guarantee a seat at busy times.

## Ways to pay

- **Darb app:** register, choose **Tickets**, pick a pass and pay by card. Show the QR code at the gate.
- **Darb card:** a reusable card sold at ticket machines and station ticket offices for SAR 10. Top it up at the machines. It also works on buses.
- **Contactless:** tap a contactless bank card, Apple Pay or Mada Pay at the gate. Use the **same card or phone to enter and exit**.

## Step by step: your first metro trip

1. Plan the route in the Darb app (or Google Maps) and note where you change lines.
2. Buy a 2-hour pass in the app, or decide which card you will tap.
3. Tap in at the gate. If you bought first class, board the first-class carriage.
4. Change lines at the interchange stations without paying again while your pass is valid.
5. Tap out at your destination with the same card or QR code.

## Buses and Park & Ride

- Riyadh buses use the same Darb tickets and passes. Use the Darb app for routes and live times.
- Several stations have **Park & Ride** car parks. Parking is free for a set time if you use the same card on the metro or bus; after that an hourly charge applies. Check the signs or the Darb app for the current limit.

## Common mistakes

- Tapping in with one card and out with another, which can charge you twice.
- Buying first class for a short off-peak trip when standard would do.
- Forgetting that a 2-hour pass starts when you activate it, not when you buy it.
