## Indian community schools (CBSE)

These schools follow India's CBSE curriculum and run under the patronage of the Embassy of India.

| City | Schools |
| --- | --- |
| Riyadh | International Indian School Riyadh; International Indian Public School Riyadh |
| Jeddah | International Indian School Jeddah |
| Dammam / Al Khobar | International Indian School Dammam (the largest, about 16,000 students) |
| Other cities | Jubail, Taif, Tabuk, Buraidah and Majma |

Many private Indian schools also run in big cities, with a wider range of fees.

## Pakistani community schools

Pakistan International Schools operate under the Pakistan government's framework for overseas schools, with campuses in cities including Riyadh, Jeddah and Buraidah. Many have:

- a **Pakistani-curriculum section**, and
- an **English section** following the UK curriculum and IGCSE.

As an example, the English section of Pakistan International School Jeddah lists fees of about SAR 10,350–23,000 a year depending on the grade.

## How to apply

1. Check the school's website or social pages for the admission window. Indian CBSE schools admit for an April start, so applications open early in the year.
2. Fill in the application form and pay any registration or assessment fee.
3. Prepare documents:
   - [ ] Child's passport and iqama copies.
   - [ ] Parents' passport and iqama copies.
   - [ ] Birth certificate.
   - [ ] Previous school report cards and transfer certificate (attested if from abroad).
   - [ ] Vaccination record and photos.
4. Attend the assessment or interview if the school asks.
5. Pay the first term's fees to confirm the seat.

## Common mistakes

- Applying after seats in popular grades fill up.
- Not getting the transfer certificate attested when moving from Pakistan or India.
- Forgetting transport fees, which can add a lot to the yearly cost.
