## Step by step

1. **Stay safe.** Check for injuries. Call **997** if anyone needs medical help.
2. **Make the road safe.** If it's minor, move the cars to a safe spot. Switch off the engine, put on hazard lights and place a warning triangle behind the car.
3. **Report it:**
   - At least one car insured → **Najm**: call 199033, use the Najm app, or WhatsApp 920000560.
   - No car insured, or a serious accident → **Moroor**: call 993.
4. **Document.** Photograph the cars, plates and road from several angles. Keep your licence, istimara and insurance details ready.
5. **Wait for the report.** Najm assesses the accident, remotely for many minor ones or with a surveyor at the scene. You'll get an SMS from Najm and Moroor.
6. **Claim insurance.** Use the Najm report to file the claim with the insurer of the driver found at fault, and follow the case in the Najm app.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving the scene before the report is done.
- Not taking photos before moving the cars.
- Signing papers you don't understand. Ask for an English explanation.
- Arguing about fault at the roadside. Najm decides.
