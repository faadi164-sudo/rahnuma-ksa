## Before you file

- [ ] Your employment contract (and Qiwa contract record).
- [ ] Salary slips and bank statements.
- [ ] Your iqama and passport copies.
- [ ] Any letters, emails and messages with the employer.
- [ ] A short, clear summary: what you are owed and since when.

## Step by step

1. **File online** on the Ministry of Human Resources' labor dispute service and choose **friendly settlement**.
2. **Describe the claim** clearly, for example: "My salary for March and April 2026 has not been paid," and attach your evidence.
3. **Attend the sessions.** The Ministry usually schedules two settlement sessions within 21 working days. They can be online.
4. **Settle, or move on.** If you agree, the settlement is recorded. If not, the Ministry refers the case to the labor court.
5. **Follow the court case on Najiz**, logging in with Nafath. Upload documents and attend hearings when called.

## Deadlines

| What | Time limit |
| --- | --- |
| Filing a claim | Within 12 months of the job ending |
| Friendly settlement | Usually completed within 21 working days |

## Getting help

- Call **19911** (Ministry of Human Resources). Help is available in several languages, including Urdu and Hindi.
- Your embassy's labor or community welfare section can also advise.

## Common mistakes

- Missing the 12-month limit.
- Going to court without the friendly settlement step. It is required first.
- Signing a settlement for less than you're owed without reading it fully.
