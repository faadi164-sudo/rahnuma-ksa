## Water and wastewater rates (monthly use)

| Monthly use | Water (SAR per m³) | Wastewater (SAR per m³) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 15 m³ | 1 | 0.50 |
| 15–30 m³ | 2 | 1 |
| 30–45 m³ | 4 | 2 |
| Above 45 m³ | 6 | 3 |

Each band is charged at its own rate.

## Fixed monthly charge (water + wastewater)

| Meter size | Charge (SAR) |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 20 mm | 30 |
| 21–40 mm | 60 |
| 41–65 mm | 90 |
| 66–100 mm | 120 |
| Above 100 mm | 150 |

Each additional home on the same meter adds SAR 30.

## Monthly bills at different levels of use

Water plus wastewater plus the SAR 30 fixed charge for a standard meter (up to 20 mm):

| Monthly use | Water (SAR) | Wastewater (SAR) | Fixed (SAR) | Total (SAR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 m³ | 15 | 7.50 | 30 | 52.50 |
| 30 m³ | 45 | 22.50 | 30 | 97.50 |
| 45 m³ | 105 | 52.50 | 30 | 187.50 |
| 60 m³ | 195 | 97.50 | 30 | 322.50 |

Doubling use from 30 to 60 m³ more than triples the bill, because every cubic metre above 45 costs SAR 6 plus SAR 3 for wastewater. A leaking tank or toilet can push a household into the top band without anyone noticing.

## What it means for expat households

- A family using 25 m³ a month would pay about SAR 35 for water (15 × 1 + 10 × 2) and SAR 17.50 for wastewater, plus the SAR 30 fixed charge.
- Check your meter size and account name in the NWC app; the meter size sets your fixed charge.
- If the account is in your landlord's name, check how water is covered in your rent.

> NWC has not said whether these rates include VAT. Check the official tariff on the NWC website.

## Checking and paying your bill

- **See the bill** in the NWC app or the NWC e-branch, registered with your iqama.
- **Pay through SADAD** in your bank app under National Water Company.
- **Put the account in your name.** NWC and Ejar link water meters to registered leases, so the bill can be issued in the tenant's name. See our [guide to the water bill](/guides/water-bill-nwc/).
- **Watch the first 2027 bills** for a sudden jump, and check for leaks before you dispute it.
