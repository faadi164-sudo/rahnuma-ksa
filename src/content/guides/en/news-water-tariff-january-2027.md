## Water and wastewater rates (monthly use)

| Monthly use | Water (SAR per m³) | Wastewater (SAR per m³) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 15 m³ | 1 | 0.50 |
| 15–30 m³ | 2 | 1 |
| 30–45 m³ | 4 | 2 |
| Above 45 m³ | 6 | 3 |

Each band is charged at its own rate.

## Fixed monthly charge (water + wastewater)

| Meter size | Charge (SAR) |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 20 mm | 30 |
| 21–40 mm | 60 |
| 41–65 mm | 90 |
| 66–100 mm | 120 |
| Above 100 mm | 150 |

Each additional home on the same meter adds SAR 30.

## What it means for expat households

- A family using 25 m³ a month would pay about SAR 35 for water (15 × 1 + 10 × 2) and SAR 17.50 for wastewater, plus the SAR 30 fixed charge.
- Check your meter size and account name in the NWC app; the meter size sets your fixed charge.
- If the account is in your landlord's name, check how water is covered in your rent.

> NWC has not said whether these rates include VAT. Check the official tariff on the NWC website.
