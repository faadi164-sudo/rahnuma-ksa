## When to scrap rather than sell

- The car was written off after an accident.
- It no longer runs and repairs cost more than it is worth.
- You are leaving on final exit and can't find a buyer in time.

## Before you start

- [ ] Your **iqama** is valid and your **Absher** account is active, with your current phone number.
- [ ] Traffic **fines are paid**.
- [ ] You know the car's **sequence number** (on the istimara).
- [ ] You have compared prices from a few scrap centres.

## Step by step

1. **Find an authorised centre.** Absher lists the centres approved for vehicle cancellation. Only these can process it.
2. **Agree the price** and hand the car over to the centre.
3. **The centre submits the cancellation request** through Absher.
4. **You receive a verification code** by SMS from Absher. Give it to the centre to approve the request. Never share the code with anyone else.
5. **Check Absher.** The car should disappear from your vehicles list.
6. **Plates go back to traffic.** The centre hands the number plates to the traffic department within a few working days.

## If you are leaving on final exit

A final exit visa can't be issued while a vehicle is registered in your name. Sell the car (and transfer ownership on Absher), or scrap and cancel it, before your employer applies for the final exit. See our final exit checklist.

## Common mistakes

- Selling a wreck for cash to someone who never cancels the registration. It stays on your iqama, along with any fines.
- Giving the Absher code before you have handed over the car and agreed the price.
- Leaving it until the last week before final exit.
