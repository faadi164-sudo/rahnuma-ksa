## Before you start

- [ ] Valid iqama and work permit.
- [ ] No unpaid traffic fines.
- [ ] Accreditation for regulated jobs, such as the Saudi Council of Engineers for engineering titles, the accounting body for accountants, or the Ministry of Agriculture for veterinary jobs.
- [ ] Your employer is not in the red Nitaqat band.

## Step by step

1. **Agree the new title with HR.** It should match your real job and qualifications.
2. **Employer submits the request** in Qiwa using the occupation change service.
3. **Approve it** in your Qiwa individual account.
4. **Fee is paid:** SAR 1,000.
5. **Iqama updated.** The new profession appears in Absher once Jawazat updates it. HR may need to complete a step in Muqeem.

## Titles you may not be able to choose

Some job titles are reserved for Saudi nationals. In January 2026, the Ministry suspended expatriates' changes into titles such as general manager, sales representative, marketing specialist and procurement manager. Expats can still be appointed general manager in certain cases, but changes to that title through Qiwa are now restricted.

## Common mistakes

- Asking for a title that doesn't match the job. A mismatch is a violation.
- Submitting before getting the required accreditation.
- Forgetting to check the new profession on your iqama after the change.
