## The seven products

| Product | Government fee | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlimited duration | SAR 800,000 once | Anyone who meets the general conditions and can pay |
| Limited duration | SAR 100,000 a year, renewable | Anyone who meets the general conditions and can pay |
| Special talent | SAR 4,000 | Executives and specialists on high salaries |
| Gifted | SAR 4,000 | Recognised talent in culture, sport and the arts |
| Investor | SAR 4,000 | Investors putting large capital into a Saudi business |
| Entrepreneur | SAR 4,000 | Founders of approved start-ups |
| Real estate owner | SAR 4,000 | Owners of qualifying residential property |

## Main benefits

- No employer sponsor needed; you can change jobs or leave a job freely.
- Your family can live with you.
- You can own residential and commercial property and run a business, within the rules.
- Exemption from some fees that apply to ordinary expats.

## Step by step

1. Check the conditions for the product you're interested in on the Premium Residency Center website.
2. Create an account and fill in the application: personal, family, education and financial details.
3. Upload your documents, such as passport, proof of income or assets, and a clean criminal record. Documents not in Arabic may need translating and attesting.
4. Pay the application fee.
5. If approved, pay the product fee and complete the residence formalities.

## Is it realistic for you?

For most salaried expats, the fee-based products are very expensive and the category products have high bars. If you earn well and plan to stay for years, compare the yearly cost of your current iqama, work permit and dependent fees with the Premium Residency fee before deciding.

## Common mistakes

- Relying on agents who promise approval. The government decides each application.
- Assuming the SAR 4,000 products have no other conditions. They all have salary, investment or property thresholds.
