## When the changes took effect

The Council of Ministers approved the amendments in August 2024 and they came into force on 19 February 2025. They revised 38 articles, removed seven and added two.

## Key changes for expat workers

| Area | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Resignation | Now formally defined. You submit it on Qiwa; it cannot carry a future date. |
| Employer response | Must accept, delay or reject within 30 days. No response means it is accepted. Delay is possible by up to 60 more days, with written reasons. |
| Withdrawing a resignation | Allowed within 7 days, unless already accepted. |
| Notice when you end an open-ended contract | 30 days (was 60). The employer's notice to you stays at 60 days. |
| Probation | Maximum 180 days. |
| Contracts for non-Saudis | Must be written and fixed-term. |
| Maternity leave | 12 weeks paid (was 10). |

## Step by step: resigning under the new rules

1. Check your contract type and end date on Qiwa.
2. Work out your end-of-service benefit first (see our end-of-service guide), because resigning reduces it.
3. Submit the resignation on Qiwa. Keep working while you wait.
4. Your employer has 30 days to respond. Watch for a written delay notice.
5. Your contract ends on the date of acceptance, after 30 days with no response, or at the end of any delay period.

## Common mistakes

- Sending a resignation by email only. Use Qiwa so the dates count.
- Stopping work as soon as you resign. You should keep working during the response period.
- Resigning without checking the effect on your end-of-service benefit.
