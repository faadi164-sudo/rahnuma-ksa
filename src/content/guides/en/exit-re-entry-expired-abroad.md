## Extension fees from abroad

| Visa type | Extension fee while you are outside Saudi Arabia |
| --- | --- |
| Single exit re-entry | SAR 200 per month (double the in-Kingdom rate) |
| Multiple exit re-entry | SAR 400 per month (double the in-Kingdom rate) |

The higher rates come from a 2022 amendment to the residency rules. The visa can only be extended while the iqama is valid, so renew the iqama first if needed.

## Step by step: extending a family member's visa

1. Make sure their iqama is valid and dependent fees are paid.
2. Pay the extension fee against their iqama number through your bank's government payments service.
3. Log in to Absher and open the services for your family members.
4. Choose the person and the extension option, set the new period and confirm.

## Step by step: extending a worker's visa

The employer does this on Absher Business (**My Business Services → Visas**) or on Muqeem. Absher Business charges its own service fee per transaction. Send your employer your iqama number and the date you need to return.

## If the visa has already expired

- You cannot enter Saudi Arabia on an expired exit re-entry visa.
- The three-year entry ban that used to apply was lifted on 16 January 2024.
- To return to the same or a new employer, you need a new work visa.
- Family members need a new visa from their sponsor.

## Tips

- Extending from abroad costs double, so choose enough months when you first issue the visa.
- Set a phone reminder two weeks before the visa's "return before" date.
- Check the visa's validity any time on Muqeem's visa validity service.
