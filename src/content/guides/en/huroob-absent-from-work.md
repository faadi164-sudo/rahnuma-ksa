## The timeline

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Employer files an absence report, or your contract ends with no new one. Status: **disconnected from work**. You and the employer get an SMS. |
| Days 1–60 | You can transfer to a new employer, leave on a final exit, or sign a new contract with the same employer. |
| Day 60 | If nothing happened, status becomes **absent from work**. You are removed from the employer's records and both ministries' systems are updated. |

## How to check your status

- The SMS sent when a report is filed.
- Your Qiwa individual account.
- Absher.
- The Ministry of Human Resources' online inquiry for non-Saudi employees, using your iqama or border number.

## Your options within the 60 days

### Transfer to a new employer

1. The new employer requests your transfer in Qiwa. Your old employer's approval is not needed.
2. You accept the request in your own Qiwa account.
3. The Ministry approves it. The transfer must then be completed within 15 days.

### Leave on a final exit

1. Pay any traffic fines first.
2. Issue the final exit visa (in Absher or through Qiwa as instructed).
3. Leave before the visa's validity ends.

### Sign a new contract with the same employer

If the report was a mistake, the employer can offer a new contract in Qiwa and you accept it.

## If the report is false

1. Don't ignore it. The 60 days run whatever you do.
2. Collect evidence: attendance records, approved leave, emails, messages, salary transfers.
3. File a case through the Ministry's friendly settlement service for labor disputes (free, within 12 months).

## Common mistakes

- Waiting for the employer to "sort it out" while the 60 days run out.
- Leaving the job without documenting it. Keep copies of resignation letters and replies.
- Paying an agent to "remove huroob". Use only Qiwa, Absher and the Ministry.
