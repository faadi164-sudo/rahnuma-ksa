## Expected holiday dates for 2027

| Holiday | Expected date | Private-sector days off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founding Day | Monday 22 February 2027 | 1 |
| Eid al-Fitr | Tuesday 9 March 2027 (1 Shawwal 1448) | 4 |
| Eid al-Adha | Arafat Day Saturday 15 May, Eid Sunday 16 May 2027 | 4, starting on Arafat Day |
| National Day | Thursday 23 September 2027 | 1 |

The Eid dates come from the Umm al-Qura calendar. Saudi Arabia confirms them after the moon sighting, so they can move by a day. The Ministry of Human Resources announces the exact private-sector dates shortly before each Eid.

## How the Eid holidays are counted

- **Eid al-Fitr:** 4 days, starting on the day after 29 Ramadan on the Umm al-Qura calendar. If the calendar holds, that gives Tuesday 9 to Friday 12 March 2027.
- **Eid al-Adha:** 4 days, starting on Arafat Day (9 Dhul Hijjah). If the calendar holds, that gives Saturday 15 to Tuesday 18 May 2027.
- **Banks:** the Saudi Central Bank publishes its own holiday dates for banks. For Eid al-Fitr 2027 it has listed 7 to 11 March.
- **Schools:** the education calendar has its own, longer breaks.

## Your rights on holidays

- Every worker has the right to **full pay** on official holidays (Article 112 of the Labor Law).
- **If you work on a holiday,** it is treated as overtime or you get a paid compensatory day.
- **If a holiday falls on your weekly day off,** an equivalent day is generally given before or after.
- **If National Day falls during an Eid holiday,** no extra day is given.
- **If you are on annual leave,** the holiday days are added to your leave rather than counted against it.
- **Hajj leave:** the Eid al-Adha holidays count towards Hajj leave (Article 114).

## Planning tips

- Flights out of Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam fill up and get expensive before both Eids. Book early, and issue your **exit re-entry visa** first.
- Check your iqama and passport validity before a holiday trip. Government offices are closed during the holidays.
- Pay bills and traffic fines before the break, as some services slow down.

## Common mistakes

- Booking non-refundable flights on the expected Eid date before the official announcement.
- Assuming the government-sector holiday applies to private-sector jobs.
- Not asking for compensation in writing when you are asked to work on Eid.
