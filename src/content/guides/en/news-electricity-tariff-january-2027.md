## The new rates (residential, before VAT)

| Monthly use | Until 31 Dec 2026 | From 1 Jan 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 6,000 kWh | 18 halalas per kWh | 21 halalas per kWh |
| Above 6,000 kWh | 30 halalas per kWh | 33 halalas per kWh |

## New fixed monthly charge

| Circuit breaker | Charge per month (SAR) |
| --- | --- |
| 20 to under 100 amps | 70 |
| 100 to under 200 amps | 80 |
| 200 to under 300 amps | 90 |
| 300 amps and above | 100 |

It replaces the old meter fee and the meter reading, maintenance and billing fee, so you don't pay both. It is calculated by the number of days in each bill. 15% VAT applies.

## What it means for expat households

- A flat using 1,500 kWh in a month pays about SAR 45 more in energy charges, plus the new fixed charge less the old fees.
- If the meter is in your name, the change shows on your own bill. If it's in the landlord's name, check how your rent or service charge covers electricity.
- Bills covering late December and early January may show both old and new rates.
