## The new rates (residential, before VAT)

| Monthly use | Until 31 Dec 2026 | From 1 Jan 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 6,000 kWh | 18 halalas per kWh | 21 halalas per kWh |
| Above 6,000 kWh | 30 halalas per kWh | 33 halalas per kWh |

## New fixed monthly charge

| Circuit breaker | Charge per month (SAR) |
| --- | --- |
| 20 to under 100 amps | 70 |
| 100 to under 200 amps | 80 |
| 200 to under 300 amps | 90 |
| 300 amps and above | 100 |

It replaces the old meter fee and the meter reading, maintenance and billing fee, so you don't pay both. It is calculated by the number of days in each bill. 15% VAT applies.

## What the energy part of a bill looks like

Energy charge only, before the fixed charge and 15% VAT:

| Monthly use | Until 31 Dec 2026 (SAR) | From 1 Jan 2027 (SAR) | Increase (SAR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 kWh | 180 | 210 | 30 |
| 2,000 kWh | 360 | 420 | 60 |
| 3,000 kWh | 540 | 630 | 90 |
| 6,000 kWh | 1,080 | 1,260 | 180 |
| 7,000 kWh | 1,380 | 1,590 | 210 |

Up to 6,000 kWh, every kWh costs 3 halalas more. Summer months with heavy air-conditioning use are where the increase shows most.

## What it means for expat households

- A flat using 1,500 kWh in a month pays about SAR 45 more in energy charges, plus the new fixed charge less the old fees.
- If the meter is in your name, the change shows on your own bill. If it's in the landlord's name, check how your rent or service charge covers electricity.
- Bills covering late December and early January may show both old and new rates.

> Reported from the Saudi Energy announcement. Check the official tariff on the Saudi Energy website.

## Paying and managing your account

- **Pay through SADAD** in your bank app. Saudi Energy is biller code 002 at most banks, and partial payments are accepted.
- **Don't let arrears build up.** Power can be disconnected once unpaid bills go above SAR 1,000.
- **Put the meter in the right name.** Ejar requires utility meters to be linked to the person using the property, and Saudi Energy asks for a meter declaration as owner or tenant. With a registered lease, the account can move into your name. See our guide to the electricity account.
- **Check the first 2027 bill** against the rates above, especially if it covers days in both December and January.
