## What can stop you coming back

| Situation | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Clean final exit, nothing on your record | No ban. Return on any new visa |
| Deported, or left through a deportation centre | Usually an entry ban. Check before applying |
| Reported as absent from work (huroob) and never cleared | Can cause problems when a new visa is checked |
| Unpaid debts, court cases or a travel ban | Can block a new visa or entry |
| Exit re-entry visa not used, never returned | No three-year ban since January 2024, but a new visa is needed |

## Step by step: coming back on a new work visa

1. **Keep your final exit record.** Save a copy of the final exit visa and your old iqama.
2. **Get a job offer.** The new employer issues the work visa through its own channels.
3. **Match your profession.** The job title on the new visa should match your qualifications, as degrees may be checked again.
4. **Complete medicals and stamping** in your home country as the visa requires.
5. **Arrive and get your new iqama.** Your employer registers you, and you get a new iqama.
6. **Register Absher and Nafath again** if your iqama number has changed, using a mobile number registered on the new iqama.

## Your old driving licence

If you held a Saudi driving licence before, book a Muroor appointment for **replacement of driving licences** after you get your new iqama. You pay the licence fee for the new period (for example SAR 400 for 10 years), plus a late fee for each year the old licence has been expired.

## Coming back on a family or visit visa

- **Family visit visa:** a relative living in Saudi Arabia applies for you through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs visa platform.
- **Tourist or other visit visas:** apply online if your nationality is eligible.
- **As a dependent:** your spouse or parent working in Saudi Arabia can bring you on a permanent family visa.

## Common mistakes

- Believing rumours of a two-year ban after a final exit. Jawazat has denied that.
- Leaving with unpaid bills, loans or fines, which can follow your record.
- Leaving without the final exit while your employer reports you as absent.
