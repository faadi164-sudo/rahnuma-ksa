## Step 1: register the birth (within 30 days)

1. Make sure you have the hospital's birth notification and that your national address is registered.
2. Log in to Absher and go to **My Services → Services → Civil Affairs**.
3. Choose **Newborn Registration**, read the requirements and fill in the application.
4. Request delivery of the birth certificate to your national address, or collect it from Civil Affairs.

## Step 2: get the baby's passport

Apply at your country's embassy or consulate in Saudi Arabia. Expect to need the Saudi birth certificate (possibly attested), photos, and both parents' passports and iqamas. Each embassy sets its own list and fees.

## Step 3: add the baby to your iqama

1. In Absher, add the newborn to your family members using the add-newborn or dependent service. Upload the documents it asks for (birth certificate, passport, photo).
2. Pay the iqama fee and the SAR 400 monthly dependent fee shown in Absher through your bank's government payments service.
3. Newborns are not exempt from the dependent fee, and it is charged from the date of birth, so expect to pay for the months since the baby was born.
4. Once the iqama is issued, the baby appears among your family members in Absher.

## Step 4: health insurance

Add the baby to your medical insurance policy through your employer or insurer.

## Common mistakes

- Missing the 30-day registration window.
- Booking travel before the iqama is issued. A newborn without an iqama cannot get an exit re-entry visa.
- Name spellings that differ between the birth certificate and the passport.
