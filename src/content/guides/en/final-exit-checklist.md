## Conditions for the visa

- Passport valid for at least 60 days.
- All entitlements received and all debts cleared, including mobile phone bills. Any unpaid amount owed to another party blocks the visa.
- No vehicle registered in your name.
- No previously issued visa left unused on your record.
- Valid iqama when the visa is issued.

The visa is valid for 60 days from issue. You must leave within that period, and it cannot be extended.

## Checklist: before the visa is issued

- [ ] Agree your last working day and get it in writing.
- [ ] Get your end-of-service benefit calculated (see our end-of-service guide).
- [ ] Pay all traffic fines on Absher.
- [ ] Sell your car or transfer its ownership.
- [ ] Settle and cancel mobile lines, and check no extra SIMs are registered on your iqama.
- [ ] Pay off or settle any bank loan or credit card.
- [ ] End your Ejar rental contract and settle electricity and water bills.
- [ ] Arrange final exit or transfer for each family member.
- [ ] Get school leaving certificates for your children, attested if needed.
- [ ] Get an experience or service certificate from your employer.

## Checklist: after the visa is issued

- [ ] Collect your final salary and end-of-service benefit. They must be paid within the deadline set by labor law.
- [ ] Close your bank account, or keep it open only if your bank allows non-residents.
- [ ] Book your flight within the 60-day window.
- [ ] Keep copies of your iqama, final exit visa and salary certificate.

## Who issues it

- **Your employer:** on Absher Business or Muqeem.
- **You:** on Absher, once your contract has ended, under the 2021 labor reforms.
- **Family members:** their sponsor issues each final exit on Absher.

## Common mistakes

- Applying while a car is still registered in your name.
- Forgetting an old, unused visa on your record.
- Leaving a phone bill unpaid; it blocks the visa.
- Missing the 60-day window, which leads to fines.
