## Find your account number

- Printed on any old water bill.
- Ask your landlord, building owner or broker.
- On NWC's e-branch, use **Know your account number** and search with your iqama number.

## Check your bill

1. Download the **National Water** app (or open NWC e-branch).
2. Register with your iqama number and mobile number.
3. Add your NWC account number (as a tenant if you rent).
4. See the current bill, due date and payment history.

## Pay your bill

1. Open your bank app → **SADAD bills**.
2. Add **National Water Company** and enter your account number.
3. Pay the full amount or a part of it.

## Put the account in your name

When the broker documents your lease in Ejar, ask for the water (and electricity) account to be transferred to you. The bill is then issued in your name and you pay NWC directly.

## Common mistakes

- Not knowing the account number until there's a problem.
- Leaving the account in the landlord's name and then disputing bills when you move out.
- Ignoring unpaid bills; services can be suspended.
