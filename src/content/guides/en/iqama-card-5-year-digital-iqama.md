## What changed

| Before | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Card validity matched your residency (often 1 year) | Card valid for 5 years |
| New card printed at every renewal | Residency renewed digitally; card stays valid |
| Collection through employer or Jawazat | Delivered by Saudi Post (Wasel) to your National Address |

## Make sure the card reaches you

1. Register or update your National Address in the SPL app.
2. Check that your mobile number in Absher is current.
3. Workers: HR requests printing and delivery through Muqeem.
4. Family members and domestic workers: request delivery in Absher under **documents delivery by mail**, choose the residence card, enter the address and confirm.

## Your digital iqama

1. Open the Absher app and log in.
2. Open your digital ID. It shows your iqama number, expiry date and a QR code.
3. Family members you sponsor can be added from the family members section.

## Common mistakes

- An old National Address, so the card goes to your previous flat.
- Thinking you don't need to renew residency because the card says 5 years.
- Not checking the expiry date of your residency in Absher, which can differ from the card's date.
