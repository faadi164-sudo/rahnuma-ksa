## Before you buy: check the car

- [ ] **Ownership and records:** ask the seller to show the car's details in Absher (owner, model year, registration expiry).
- [ ] **Fahas:** the periodic inspection should be valid. Ask for a recent report, or take the car for inspection.
- [ ] **Mechanical check:** have a workshop you trust inspect it.
- [ ] **Accident history:** ask for any Najm accident reports.
- [ ] **Registration (istimara):** check its expiry date. Renewing costs SAR 100 a year.

## Step by step: transfer on Absher

### Seller

1. Log in to Absher and open the **Vehicle Sale** service under vehicles.
2. Choose the car and enter the buyer's iqama number, the price and your IBAN.
3. Submit the request.

### Buyer

1. Log in to Absher and accept the sale request.
2. Transfer the price into the escrow account shown, plus the SAR 230 service fee (including VAT).
3. Buy vehicle insurance in your own name. It links to Absher automatically.
4. Pay the SAR 150 ownership transfer fee through your bank (government payments → traffic → transfer of ownership).
5. When all steps are done, the car moves to your name and the seller receives the money.

## Fees

| Fee | Amount (SAR) | Usually paid by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership transfer (private car) | 150 | Buyer |
| Absher Vehicle Sale service (incl. VAT) | 230 | Buyer |

## Common mistakes

- Handing over the keys before Absher shows the car in the buyer's name.
- Paying cash outside escrow.
- Forgetting insurance in the buyer's name. The transfer won't finish without it.
- Buying a car with unpaid fines. Check them before you agree the price.
