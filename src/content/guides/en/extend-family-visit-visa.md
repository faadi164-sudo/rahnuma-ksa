A family visit visa lets your parents, in-laws or other relatives stay with you for a set period. If they want to stay longer, you, as the host, extend it online. Jawazat says there is no need to visit its offices.

## The rules in short

| Rule | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| When | From seven days before the visa expires |
| Where | The host's Absher account (individuals or business) |
| Fee | Paid before you extend; Jawazat has described it as SAR 100 for each monthly extension |
| Insurance | The visitor must have valid medical insurance for the new period |
| Maximum stay | 180 days in total from the date of entry, including all extensions |
| Late | Possible up to three days after expiry if the visitor is still in Saudi Arabia, but a fine applies |
| Visitor's location | The visitor must be inside Saudi Arabia |

## Before you extend

1. **Check the dates.** Note the visa expiry and the date your visitor entered. Work out how many days are left before the 180-day limit.
2. **Sort out insurance.** The visitor needs valid medical insurance covering the extra time. Buy or extend a policy from a licensed insurer before you start. Absher checks it when you extend.
3. **Pay the fee.** Pay the visit visa extension fee through the government payments (SADAD) section of your bank app. Absher won't extend the visa until the fee is paid.

## Step by step in Absher

1. Log in to Absher with your own account (the visitor's host).
2. Go to **Services**, then **Family Members Services**.
3. Choose **Extend Visit Visa**, then **Proceed**.
4. Find your visitor in the list and select **Extend Visa**.
5. Check the details, accept the terms and confirm with **Extend Visit Visa**.
6. Note the new expiry date that Absher shows, and save or screenshot it.

## About the medical insurance

Visit visa insurance covers emergencies only, up to SAR 100,000 per visitor, from the day they enter Saudi Arabia. It isn't a full health policy, so routine treatment is paid separately. Every extension needs cover for the new period, so check the end date of the policy before each extension. You can check a visitor's policy with the Insurance Authority's **Visit Visa Travel Insurance Inquiry**, using the passport number the visa was issued on. See our [guide to visit visa medical insurance](/guides/visit-visa-medical-insurance/).

## Single-entry and multiple-entry visas

- **Single-entry visa:** extend in Absher as above, as many times as needed, while the total stay stays within 180 days of entry.
- **Multiple-entry visa:** Jawazat has said holders of multiple-entry family visit visas must leave the country after three months rather than extend. They can return on the same visa while it is still valid.

## If you miss the date

Jawazat allows the extension up to three days after the visa expires, as long as the visitor is still in Saudi Arabia. A fine is imposed for extending late; one expat site puts the late fee at SAR 500. After that window, don't let the visitor stay on. Contact Jawazat straight away instead of waiting.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until the last day. Start the extension a week before expiry.
- Letting the insurance lapse. The extension fails if the policy doesn't cover the new period.
- Counting from the visa issue date. The 180-day limit counts from the day your visitor entered Saudi Arabia.
- Planning a long stay on a visit visa for a wife or children. Use the [permanent family visa](/guides/permanent-family-visa/) for them instead.
