## Route 1: visa on arrival

Jawazat has said a residents' newborn can be given a visa at the point of entry if:

- the baby has their own passport,
- both parents are residents of Saudi Arabia, and
- the mother is on her husband's sponsorship.

**Carry these documents** (originals, plus copies):

- [ ] Baby's passport
- [ ] Birth certificate
- [ ] Vaccination card
- [ ] Both parents' iqamas and passports
- [ ] The mother's exit re-entry visa

> Tell the check-in staff and the immigration officer that you are travelling with a resident's newborn for a visa on arrival, and ask for the baby's border number to be linked to the father's record.

## Route 2: visa stamped before travel

If you don't meet the conditions above, or want certainty before you fly, apply for the baby's visa through the Saudi embassy or its authorised visa centre in your country. Expect to need the baby's passport, an attested birth certificate, photos, the vaccination card, the parents' passport and iqama copies, and the mother's exit re-entry visa. Check the embassy's current list before you apply.

## After you arrive

1. Note the baby's **border number** from the entry stamp or visa.
2. Log in to Absher and add the baby to the father's dependents using the newborn or add-dependent service. Upload the documents it asks for.
3. Pay the iqama issuance fee and the dependent fee shown in Absher through your bank's government payments service.
4. Collect the iqama, or request delivery by post through Absher.

## Common mistakes

- Travelling before the baby's passport is issued.
- The mother's own exit re-entry visa expiring while she waits for the baby's documents.
- Forgetting the vaccination card.
