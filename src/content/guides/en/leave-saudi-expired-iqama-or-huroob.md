> **Get help early.** The longer an iqama stays expired, the bigger the fines. Don't pay "agents" who promise an exit without the official steps.

## Why you can't just get a final exit

Jawazat has confirmed that the rules require a **valid iqama** before a final exit visa can be issued. The same applies to cancelling a final exit that expired unused, which also carries a SAR 1,000 fine. So the first job is almost always to make the iqama valid again, or to move to an employer who will.

## Your options, from best to last resort

| Option | When it fits | Main points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Employer renews, then issues a final exit** | Your employer is cooperative | Employer pays the late iqama fines, renews, then issues the final exit on Absher Business or Muqeem |
| **2. Transfer to a new employer** | You want to stay and work | Since 2026, a transfer is possible with an expired iqama if the new employer starts it in Qiwa and pays the late fines |
| **3. Ministry of Human Resources** | Employer refuses, owes you wages, or reported you absent unfairly | File through friendly settlement; the Ministry or labor court can order your dues and help resolve your status |
| **4. Your embassy** | You can't reach your employer, or have no job | Embassy welfare sections help nationals in irregular status; some, like India's, run online forms for final exit help |
| **5. Self-Deportation Platform** | You have no valid status and want to leave | Jawazat's digital route to complete exit formalities without being detained first |
| **6. Deportation centre** | Arrest, or no other route | Leads to removal and, usually, an entry ban |

## If you are reported absent (huroob)

- Check your status in Qiwa or Absher first.
- Within **60 days** of being marked "disconnected from work", you can still transfer, take a final exit or sign a new contract. See our huroob guide.
- If the report is false, object through the Ministry's labor dispute service.
- Rules have been relaxed in 2026: workers marked absent who have been in Saudi Arabia for more than 12 months can transfer to a new employer through Qiwa.

## About the Self-Deportation Platform

The Ministry of Interior's General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat) announced the platform in November 2025 for people who violate residency, labor or border rules. It lets them complete exit procedures online instead of going through detention first. Using it does not mean fines or an entry ban are waived. Check the conditions on Jawazat's official channels before you start, and use only official government websites.

## Step by step: if your employer is cooperative

1. Ask HR to renew your iqama and pay the late fines.
2. Check on Absher that the new expiry date shows.
3. Settle your bills, fines and any car in your name (see our final exit checklist).
4. Your employer issues the final exit. Leave within its validity.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting months hoping the problem goes away; fines grow.
- Paying a middleman for a "special" exit.
- Stopping work without documenting unpaid salary, which weakens your case.
- Choosing deportation when a renewal or transfer was still possible.
