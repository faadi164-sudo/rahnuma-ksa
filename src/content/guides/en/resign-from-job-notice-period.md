## Notice periods (after the 2025 amendments)

| Situation | Notice |
| --- | --- |
| You resign (indefinite contract) | 30 days |
| You resign (fixed-term contract) | Written resignation; deemed accepted after 30 days without reply |
| Employer ends an indefinite contract | 60 days |
| During probation | Either side can end it without notice |

## Step by step: resign properly

1. **Check your contract in Qiwa:** fixed-term or indefinite, end date and notice clause.
2. **Write your resignation** with the date and your last working day. Submit it in writing (email or letter, or through Qiwa where your employer uses it) and keep proof of the date.
3. **Count 30 days.** On a fixed-term contract, if the employer doesn't respond in 30 days, the resignation is accepted. The employer can push acceptance back by up to 60 days for work reasons, but only with written justification before the 30 days end.
4. **Work your notice.** Your salary continues during it.
5. **Collect what you're owed:** final salary, unused leave, and end-of-service award (within two weeks of a resignation).
6. **Next step:** transfer to a new employer in Qiwa, or final exit.

## If the employer breaks the contract

Under Article 81 you can leave without notice and keep your full rights if the employer seriously breaches the contract, for example by not paying wages or abusing you. Document everything and use the Ministry's friendly settlement service if needed.

## Common mistakes

- Resigning by WhatsApp only, with no record of the date.
- Leaving before the notice ends, which can trigger a huroob report.
- Not checking your end-of-service amount before signing a final settlement.
