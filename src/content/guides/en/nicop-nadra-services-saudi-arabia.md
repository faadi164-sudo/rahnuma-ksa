## Option 1: Pak-ID app (online)

1. Download the **Pak Identity (Pak-ID)** app from NADRA.
2. Register with your email and mobile number.
3. Choose NICOP (new, renewal, modification or reprint).
4. Upload photo, fingerprints and signature as the app guides you, plus supporting documents.
5. Pay online and track the application. The card is couriered to you.

| Category | Fee (Gulf countries, online) |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | USD 20 |
| Urgent | USD 30 |
| Executive | USD 40 |

## Option 2: NADRA desk at the embassy or consulate

- **Riyadh:** NADRA office at the Pakistan Embassy (Diplomatic Quarter) handles NICOP, CNIC, children's registration (CRC) and family registration certificates (FRC).
- **Jeddah:** Consulate General NADRA desk handles NICOP and SNICOP renewal, reprint, modification and FRC. **Since 1 October 2026, appointments only:** book online at the consulate's appointment portal; you get an SMS and tracking ID.

**Bring:** current passport and iqama (originals and copies), old CNIC or NICOP, and photos if asked.

## Common mistakes

- Turning up in Jeddah without an appointment after 1 October 2026.
- Letting the NICOP expire before renewing your passport online. Passport renewal checks your NADRA record.
- Using agents for something the Pak-ID app does directly.
