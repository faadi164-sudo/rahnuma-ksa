## What a National Address contains

| Part | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Short address | 4 letters and 4 digits, such as ABCD1234 |
| Building number | 4 digits |
| Street and district | Your street and district names |
| City and postal code | Your city and its 5-digit postal code |
| Additional number | 4 digits, written after the postal code (for example 12345-6789) |

## Step by step: register

1. Download the official **SPL** app (Saudi Post) or open splonline.com.sa.
2. Log in with **Nafath**. Approve the request in the Nafath app.
3. Open **National Address** and choose **Add address**.
4. Allow location access and pin your building on the map.
5. Enter the building number and choose the address type (residential).
6. Review and confirm. Your short address and full National Address appear in the app.

## Add your family

1. In the SPL app or website, open your National Address.
2. Choose to add dependents and select the family members on your iqama.

## After you move

1. Add the new address in the SPL app.
2. Make it your main address, then delete the old one. You must always keep at least one address.
3. Update your bank and any delivery apps with the new short address.

## Common mistakes

- Pinning the wrong building. Check the building number on the plate outside.
- Forgetting to update it after moving, so iqama cards and parcels go to the old flat.
- Using a P.O. Box code instead of your National Address postal code for deliveries.
