## Route 1: your country is on the approved list

### Before you start

- [ ] Valid iqama and an active Absher account.
- [ ] Valid original foreign licence.
- [ ] Certified Arabic translation of the licence, if the traffic office asks for it.
- [ ] 2–4 passport photos with a white background.

### Step by step

1. **Medical check.** Do the driving medical (eye test, blood group, general check) at an Efada-approved centre. The result links to your Absher account.
2. **Pay the fee.** In your bank app: government payments (SADAD) → traffic → driving licence issuance. SAR 40 a year, for 2, 5 or 10 years.
3. **Book an appointment.** In Absher, open traffic services and book an appointment at your nearest Moroor (traffic department) office.
4. **Attend.** Bring your iqama, passport, original licence, translation and photos. No driving test is normally needed.
5. **Collect your licence.** It is often issued the same day. The digital licence also appears in Absher.

## Route 2: your country is not on the list (including Pakistan and India)

1. **Medical check** at an Efada-approved centre.
2. **Book a driving school** in Absher (traffic services) and open a file at the school.
3. **Training.** The school decides your course. A full beginner course is about SAR 2,500–3,000; experienced drivers may get a shorter course for about SAR 1,000–1,500.
4. **Theory test** on computer, booked through Absher.
5. **Practical test**, booked through Absher after the theory test.
6. **Pay the licence fee** (SAR 40 a year) and collect the licence.

## What it costs

| Item | Approved list | Driving school route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Efada medical | About SAR 150 | About SAR 150 |
| Licence fee (10 years) | SAR 400 | SAR 400 |
| Translation | About SAR 100 | Not needed |
| Driving school course | — | About SAR 1,000–3,000 |

> Costs other than the SAR 40-a-year licence fee are estimates from expat guides. Schools and medical centres set their own prices.

## Common mistakes

- Paying for a translation before checking that your country is on the list.
- Booking the appointment before the medical result shows in Absher.
- Driving on a foreign licence long after your iqama is issued. Driving without a valid licence is a traffic violation.
