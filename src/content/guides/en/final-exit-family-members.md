## Before you start

- [ ] Each family member's iqama is valid (renew it first if it has expired).
- [ ] Dependent fees are fully paid.
- [ ] Your traffic fines are paid. Unpaid fines block final exit for your family.
- [ ] No car or other vehicle is registered in the family member's name.
- [ ] Any bills or debts in their name are cleared.

## Step by step

1. Log in to Absher Individuals as the head of family.
2. Go to **Family Members** and open **Visa Services**.
3. Choose the family member who is leaving.
4. Select **Final Exit** as the visa type and confirm.
5. Print or save the visa. It's free.
6. Book the flight within the visa's validity and travel.

## After they leave

- Their iqama is cancelled and they disappear from your family list in Absher.
- Remove them from your National Address and health insurance.
- Keep a copy of the final exit visa and boarding pass in case you need proof later.

## If plans change

- **Not travelling?** Cancel the final exit in Absher while the iqama is still valid.
- **Visa expired without leaving?** A SAR 1,000 fine applies. Contact Jawazat to resolve it before doing anything else in Absher.

## Common mistakes

- Issuing the visa before booking travel, then running out of time.
- Forgetting a car registered in your wife's name.
- Leaving yourself on a final exit without arranging your family first. When the head of family leaves on final exit, dependents normally leave too.
