## What the policy covers

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Emergency medical treatment | Up to SAR 100,000 per visitor for the policy period |
| Emergency pregnancy and delivery | Up to SAR 5,000 |
| Emergency dental treatment | Up to SAR 500 |
| Routine check-ups, regular medicines, non-emergency care | Not covered |

Limits come from the Insurance Authority's published answers and policy schedules. Your policy document lists the exact benefits, the insurer and its approved hospitals.

## Step by step: check the policy (do this on arrival day)

1. Open the Insurance Authority's **Visit Visa Travel Insurance Inquiry** service on ia.gov.sa and choose **Start service**.
2. Enter the **passport number used for the visa** (the form calls it the identity number).
3. Agree to the terms and search.
4. Save the insurer's name, the policy number and the end date. Download the policy certificate from the insurer's website if it offers one.

## Step by step: in an emergency

1. Go to the emergency department of a hospital, preferably one in the insurer's network.
2. Tell the staff the patient is on a **visit visa with visitor medical insurance**, and give the passport and policy details.
3. Ask the hospital to send the case to the insurer for approval straight away.
4. Keep every paper: medical reports, prescriptions, invoices, receipts, approval or rejection messages and any reference numbers.
5. If you had to pay a deposit, ask the hospital to claim from the insurer and refund you once the claim is approved.

## If the claim is refused

1. Ask the insurer for the reason in writing.
2. File a **Complaint against an insurance company** on the Insurance Authority website, attaching the documents above.
3. Unresolved disputes go to the insurance disputes committees.

## Extending the visit

A visit visa can only be extended if the insurance covers the new period. Buy or extend the policy first, check it shows on the inquiry service, then extend the visa on Absher.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until the hospital desk to check whether the policy exists.
- Assuming the policy covers ongoing treatment for a known illness.
- Extending the visa before the new insurance appears in the system.
- Throwing away receipts, which you need for any claim or complaint.
