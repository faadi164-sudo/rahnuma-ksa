## What the colours mean

| Band | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Platinum | Top compliance. Services run smoothly |
| Green (high, medium, low) | Compliant. Renewals and transfers work normally; low green has some limits |
| Yellow | Below target. Some services restricted |
| Red | Non-compliant. No new visas or work permit renewals; you can transfer out without consent |

## How to check

1. Open the Ministry of Human Resources' inquiry service for non-Saudi employees.
2. Enter your iqama (or border or passport) number, nationality and the verification code.
3. The result shows your employer's band, your work permit number and employment status.

You can also see your employer's details in your Qiwa individual account.

## If your employer is red

1. Check when your iqama and work permit expire.
2. Ask HR when the company expects to leave red.
3. If you need to renew soon, look for a job with a green or platinum employer. It can request your transfer in Qiwa without your current employer's approval.

## Common mistakes

- Thinking the colour is about you.
- Waiting until your iqama expires before checking.
- Accepting a job offer from a company that is itself in red.
