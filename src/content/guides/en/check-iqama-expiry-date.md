## Method 1: the Absher app (your own iqama)

1. Open the Absher app and log in.
2. Open your digital ID. The iqama expiry date is shown on it.

## Method 2: Absher iqama expiry inquiry (any iqama)

1. Log in to Absher Individuals on the website or app.
2. Find the **iqama expiry inquiry** service.
3. Enter the iqama number and the image code.
4. Select **View**. The expiry date appears.

## Method 3: without Absher (Ministry of Human Resources)

1. Open the Ministry of Human Resources individuals portal and choose **New user**.
2. Accept the privacy notice.
3. Enter your iqama number, date of birth and the verification code.
4. Enter the code sent to your mobile.
5. Your record appears, including your residency (iqama) date.

## After you check

- Expiring within a month? Remind your employer, or renew your family's iqamas yourself (see our renewal guide).
- Already expired? Renew as soon as possible. Fines start three days after expiry: SAR 500 the first time, SAR 1,000 the second.
