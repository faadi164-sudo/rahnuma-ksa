## Method 1: the Absher app (your own iqama)

1. Open the Absher app and log in.
2. Open your digital ID. The iqama expiry date is shown on it.

## Method 2: Absher iqama expiry inquiry (any iqama)

1. Log in to Absher Individuals on the website or app.
2. Find the **iqama expiry inquiry** service.
3. Enter the iqama number and the image code.
4. Select **View**. The expiry date appears.

## Method 3: without Absher (Ministry of Human Resources)

1. Open the Ministry of Human Resources individuals portal and choose **New user**.
2. Accept the privacy notice.
3. Enter your iqama number, date of birth and the verification code.
4. Enter the code sent to your mobile.
5. Your record appears, including your residency (iqama) date.

## The card date isn't your residency date

The plastic iqama card is now valid for five years, but your residency itself is still renewed on its usual cycle, often for 3, 6, 9 or 12 months at a time. So the date printed on a new card can be years after the date your residency actually runs out. Always go by the expiry shown in Absher or in the checks above.

New cards are delivered by Wasel to your registered National Address, and you don't need to carry one at all: Jawazat accepts the digital iqama on your phone.

If you sponsor your family, their iqamas are often renewed every three months, with the SAR 400 monthly dependent fee paid at each renewal. Check each family member's date, not just yours.

## What an expired iqama blocks

- **Exit re-entry visas.** The visa can only run within your iqama's validity, so an expired iqama means no trip out and back.
- **Final exit.** Jawazat has confirmed that a final exit visa needs a valid iqama, so renew it before leaving for good.
- **Fines.** Late renewal is fined from three days after expiry: SAR 500 the first time, SAR 1,000 the second, and deportation the third.
- **Changing jobs.** Since 2026, a transfer can still go ahead with an expired iqama if the new employer starts it in Qiwa and pays the late fines.

## After you check

- Expiring within a month? Remind your employer, or renew your family's iqamas yourself (see our renewal guide).
- Already expired? Renew as soon as possible. Fines start three days after expiry: SAR 500 the first time, SAR 1,000 the second.
