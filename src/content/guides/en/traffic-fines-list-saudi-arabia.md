## Fine ranges

| Range (SAR) | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| 100–150 | Driving without valid vehicle insurance; parking where it isn't allowed |
| 150–300 | Not wearing a seatbelt; not carrying your licence or registration card |
| 300–500 | Driving with an expired licence; not staying in your lane |
| 500–900 | Not stopping fully at a STOP sign |
| 1,000–2,000 | No motorcycle helmet; stopping on railway tracks; more passengers than the licence allows |
| 3,000–6,000 | Running a red light; driving the wrong way; overtaking a stopped school bus; no number plates |
| 5,000–10,000 | Driving under the influence; road works without notifying the authorities |

> Older lists put driving the wrong way at SAR 500–900. Moroor's own statements in 2023 and 2025 put it at SAR 3,000–6,000, which is what we show.

## Speeding

Speeding fines run from SAR 150 to SAR 2,000 depending on how far over the limit you were and the road's limit. On 140 km/h highways, a smaller margin over the limit puts you in a higher band.

| Over the limit (roads up to 120 km/h) | Fine (SAR) |
| --- | --- |
| 10–20 km/h | 150–300 |
| 20–30 km/h | 300–500 |
| 30–40 km/h | 800–1,000 |
| 40–50 km/h | 1,200–1,500 |
| More than 50 km/h | 1,500–2,000 |

## Drifting (tafheet)

| Offence | Fine (SAR) | Also |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | 20,000 | Vehicle impounded |
| Second | 40,000 | Vehicle impounded |
| Third | 60,000 | Vehicle can be confiscated; court referral |

## Violations that don't get the 25% discount

Moroor listed nine exclusions, including drifting, accidents caused by overtaking or speeding, vehicle weight, size or inspection violations, and workshop and showroom violations.

## Before you rely on any list

1. Open Absher, go to **Traffic Services → Traffic violations**, and check the amount recorded for you.
2. If you think the violation is wrong, object within 30 days.
3. Pay only through Absher, Efaa or your bank's government payments service.
