## How the status changes with age

| Age | Status | What you pay | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Under 18 | Dependent | SAR 400 a month | Normal family iqama |
| 18 to 24 | Companion | SAR 400 a month | Can stay under your sponsorship |
| 25 and over | Must transfer | — | Transfer to an employer (or leave) before the next renewal |

## Your options when your son turns 18

1. **Keep him as a companion.** Renew his iqama as usual and keep paying SAR 400 a month.
2. **He gets a job.** The employer transfers his sponsorship and issues a work permit.
3. **He studies abroad.** Keep his iqama renewed if he'll return during holidays; otherwise consider a final exit to stop the fee.
4. **Final exit.** Issue it in Absher if he is leaving for good. See our guide on final exit for family members.

## Plan ahead for 25

- Start looking for a job and employer sponsorship well before his 25th birthday.
- Without a transfer, Jawazat won't renew his iqama after 25.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming the fee stops at 18. It continues for companions.
- Leaving the age-25 transfer to the last month.
- Letting him work without a work permit while on your iqama.
