## Option 1: renew online (DGIP portal)

**Who can use it:** holders of a machine-readable Pakistani passport that has expired or has less than one year of validity left.

1. Open the DGIP online passport portal and create an account with your email and mobile number.
2. Choose renewal, then pick pages (36, 72 or 100), validity (5 or 10 years) and normal or urgent.
3. Upload colour scans of your current passport (the first two pages and any page the system asks for) and your iqama.
4. Upload a photo and signature, and fingerprints if asked, following the portal's specifications.
5. Pay the fee online by card.
6. Track the status in your account. The new passport is sent to the mission you selected for collection.

## Option 2: renew at the mission

1. Check the Pakistan Embassy Riyadh or Consulate General Jeddah website for appointments and opening hours.
2. Bring the original and a copy of your passport, your CNIC or NICOP, and a copy of your iqama.
3. For children under 18: the CRC (B-form), NICOP or Smart card, and copies of both parents' CNICs. For a newborn, add the birth certificate.
4. Pay the fee at the counter and keep the receipt for collection.

> Schedule reported in April 2025. Confirm the current fee with the mission or on the portal before you pay.

## After you get the new passport

- [ ] Update the new passport number in Absher (your employer does this for your own record).
- [ ] Update your bank, telecom provider and insurer if they hold your passport number.
- [ ] Keep the old cancelled passport. It may carry visas or stamps you need later.

## Common mistakes

- Applying online with an expired NICOP.
- Photos that don't meet the portal's size and background rules.
- Booking an exit re-entry visa before the new passport is updated in Absher.
